# Valley Pawn — City Community Knowledge Base
**Built 2026-08-22.** Source of truth for Lane C (Community) content. Every store's page speaks
its own town, in its own town's words.

> **Why this file exists.** The 90-day audit found Community posts were the single best-performing
> organic content we produce (Skyline Drive, Walker Street, named employees all landed in the top
> 5) — and the batch skipped the Community pillar entirely whenever anything else degraded, because
> Community was generated inside the same run as product posts. Lane C is now independent, and this
> is the raw material it draws from so it never has to invent anything.

## The three hard rules for every Community post

1. **No CTA. No product. No price. No "come see us."** The voice test from `PILLAR_OVERLAY`:
   *if a stranger would read it as "a local business that loves its town," it's right. If it reads
   as marketing, rewrite or kill it.*
2. **Never name a competitor. Never name a private individual. No politics, no crime.**
3. **A wrong local detail is worse than no post.** Confidence tags below are load-bearing:
   `[C26]` = confirmed 2026 date, safe to publish dated · `[PATTERN]` = the tradition is real, the
   2026 date is not published — write about the tradition, **never state a date** ·
   `[VERIFY]` = do not publish without a fresh check.

## Cooldowns (carried from PILLAR_OVERLAY)
- **45-day hook cooldown** — no landmark/angle reused inside 45 days on the same page.
- Community runs **3–4 posts/week**, routed store-local so each town sees its own town.

## CULPEPER — 571 James Madison Hwy
**Identity: Piedmont, not Valley.** The crossroads/gateway town between the DC exurbs and the Blue
Ridge. Never call Culpeper a Shenandoah Valley town — locals will notice.

**Landmarks:** Davis Street (National Trust *Great American Main Street* winner 2012; surveyed by a
17-year-old George Washington, 1749–59) · **the Depot** (1904 Southern Railway station, 111 Commerce
St — still a working Amtrak stop, not a museum piece) · Yowell Meadow Park · Lake Culpeper (255
acres; *note: Mountain Run Lake is a different water body — do not conflate*) · **Library of
Congress Packard Campus** and the Mt. Pony Theater, the national film/audio archive built into a
Cold War currency bunker — genuinely unique in the five-store footprint · Cedar Mountain Battlefield
· Culpeper County Courthouse (1870–74) · "The Trailblazers" mural on E. Davis, honoring the historic
Black community of The Wharf and Fishtown · Round Hill Farm pumpkin patch (~Sept 28–Oct 31).

**Local words:** "the Depot" · "The Wharf" (lower E. Davis) · "Fishtown" (named for the Friday fish
fries) · "Tin Cup Alley" (E. Spencer St) · "Slabtown" · locals firmly distinguish **Town of
Culpeper** from **Culpeper County**.

**Schools:** Culpeper County HS — **Blue Devils** (blue/gold) · Eastern View HS — **Cyclones**
(Columbia blue/black/silver) · Germanna CC Daniel Technology Center.

**Seasonal:** Piedmont foliage peaks **late Oct into early Nov — later than the mountain towns**,
which is a genuinely good angle. First frost mid-to-late Oct.

**Dates Aug–Dec 2026:** Fall Restaurant Week Sept 29–Oct 5 `[C26]` · **Culpeper Air Fest Sat Oct 10**
(26th yr; Night Lights Fri Oct 9) `[C26]` · Hop N Hog Block Party Sat Oct 17 at the Depot `[C26]` ·
Mountain Run Winery Fall Festival Sat Oct 17 `[C26]` · Farmers market final day Sat Oct 31 `[C26]` ·
downtown trick-or-treat Oct 31 `[PATTERN]` · Veterans Day at Culpeper National Cemetery Nov 11
`[PATTERN]` · Holiday Open House + tree lighting at the LOVE sign ~Sun Nov 22 `[PATTERN]` · Winter
farmers market first 3 Saturdays of Dec `[PATTERN]` · **Christmas Parade likely Sun Dec 6, 5pm
`[VERIFY]`** · Wreaths Across America Sat Dec 19 noon `[C26]`.
**Thin weeks:** Sept 14, Sept 21, Nov 2–21 → lean on foliage-peaks-later and the Depot.
**Do not post as upcoming:** CulpeperFest (June), Downtown Carnival (Feb). **There is no Culpeper
Oktoberfest** — Hop N Hog is the fall beer/BBQ event.

## WAYNESBORO — 1321 W Broad St
**Identity: AT trail town, Milepost 0, and a made-things-here manufacturing past.** The arc is
working town → river-and-arts town.

**Landmarks:** **the South River** (locals just say "the river") · South River Greenway · Ridgeview
Park · Constitution Park · Coyner Springs Park · **Wayne Theatre** (521 W. Main; 2026–27 season is
titled *"For the Love"*) · the **Street Arts Trail** — *Kaiya with Tulips* by Nils Westergard on the
old ice factory (ranked among the world's best murals), *Poochie*, *The Lovers* by Julia Chon at the
YMCA · **Rockfish Gap / Afton Mountain** (1,903 ft) where **Skyline Drive ends and the Blue Ridge
Parkway begins at Milepost 0**, and the AT crosses — the single best geography fact we own ·
Humpback Rocks (Parkway MP 5.8, outcrop ~3,080 ft) · **official ATC Appalachian Trail Community** —
hiker hostels, a hiker campsite on Race Ave, free YMCA showers · P. Buckley Moss Museum (free) ·
Shenandoah Valley Art Center · **Basic City**, the 1890s steel boomtown that merged in 1923–24.

**Local words:** "Afton" / "the Gap" · "the Parkway" (they treat MP 0 as theirs) · "Basic City" ·
"the river" · "the Boro."

**Schools:** Waynesboro HS — **Little Giants** (purple/Vegas gold), Shenandoah District. Rivals:
Wilson Memorial, Fort Defiance, Stuarts Draft, Buffalo Gap, Riverheads. BRCC Waynesboro Outpost at
1010 E. Main.

**Seasonal:** Gap colors early-to-mid Oct, valley floor mid-to-late Oct. **Afton Mountain fog and
ice on I-64** is a universally understood local rhythm — VDOT runs a dedicated fog warning system,
and locals say "the mountain's socked in." High-resonance, zero-risk seasonal content.

**Dates Aug–Dec 2026:** Craft Sublime Harvest Market Weds through Sept 30 `[PATTERN]` · Boro Fiddlen'
Folk Festival Sat Sept 12 `[C26]` · **Virginia Fall Foliage Art Show, 55th annual, Sat–Sun Oct 10–11**
`[C26]` · Fall Foliage 10K/5K early-to-mid Oct `[PATTERN]` · Farmers market final day Sat Oct 31
`[C26]` · trick-or-treat Oct 31 `[VERIFY]` · **holiday parade + tree lighting `[VERIFY]`**.
> ⚠️ **The circulating "Nov 21 lighting / Nov 22 parade" is 2025 data.** Main Street Waynesboro's
> 2026 parade application deadline is Tue Nov 24, which puts the parade *after* Thanksgiving —
> most likely the first weekend of December. **Publish no Waynesboro holiday date** until
> mainstreetwaynesboro.org posts it.
> ⚠️ **Search hazard:** Waynesboro, *Pennsylvania* has its own tree lighting. Anything from
> explorefranklincountypa.com is the wrong town.

**Thin weeks:** Nov 2–30 → Afton fog, first snow on the mountain, Parkway MP 0.

## HARRISONBURG — 1790 E Market St STE 22
**Identity: Rocktown / the Friendly City.** Big college town, mountain-bike town, food town, and the
most culturally diverse of the five.

**Landmarks:** **Court Square** and the Rockingham County Courthouse (1896–97, domed clock tower
topped by Justice; fifth courthouse on the site since 1778) with the historic springhouse in the SW
corner · the **downtown Art Walk** — ~30 murals/mosaics/sculptures, including the Lucy Simms mural
honoring the educator who taught 1,800+ students over 56 years, and a four-story chess-player mural
(*official name is "Art Walk," not "mural trail"*) · Turner Pavilion & Park · Hillandale Park,
gateway to **Rocktown Trails** · Purcell Park · Westover Park (21-hole disc golf) · **Blacks Run**,
the six-mile creek through downtown, and the Northend Greenway · **Reddish Knob** — 4,397 ft, up the
one-lane Briery Branch Rd, a real local pilgrimage for sunrise, sunset and stargazing (*spelling:
Reddish, one word, two d's*) · Switzer/Skidmore Lake · JMU's Edith J. Carrier Arboretum (125 acres)
and **the Quad** · Explore More Discovery Museum · the Dayton Market · Massanutten.

> ⚠️ **Court Square Theater closed permanently Dec 31, 2025.** Never reference it as active.

**Local words:** "Rocktown" · "the Burg" · "the Friendly City" · "Court Square" · "the Dukes" /

**Seasonal:** Foliage mid-to-late Oct. **First frost earliest of the five: Oct 1–15.** Poultry is the
county's economic heartbeat — Rockingham leads Virginia in agricultural production. JMU game day
dominates the fall calendar.

## LEXINGTON — 125 Walker St
**Identity: a town of ~7,000 with two colleges side by side** — a state military institute and a
private liberal-arts university — sharing one walkable historic downtown.

> ⚠️ **HARD EXCLUSION.** Stay entirely off Confederate memorial history, Lee Chapel, and
> Lee-Jackson Day. There is no version of that content worth the risk.

**Landmarks:** Main St (part of the colonial Great Wagon Road) and Washington St (the arts block) ·
the **textured brick sidewalks**, made at the Chilhowie Brick Plant 1880–1910 and deliberately
textured for traction on the hills — a lovely, verifiable, very "we notice our town" detail ·
Rockbridge County Courthouse · Alexander-Withrow House (1789) · **VMI Parade Ground** — cadet dress
parades most Friday afternoons, open to the public (Fall 2026: Oct 4, Oct 18 10:30a, Oct 24, Oct 31,
Nov 7 4:30p) · the Colonnade at W&L · **Chessie Nature Trail**, ~7 miles along the Maury on the old
C&O right-of-way to Buena Vista · Woods Creek Trail · **Goshen Pass** (locals say "Goshen") · House
Mountain (3,645 ft) and Jump Mountain (swinging bridge over the Maury) · **Hull's Drive-In** — 319
spaces, **one of only two nonprofit community-owned drive-ins in the country**, running since 1950,
season ~late March to end of October `[VERIFY closing weekend]` · **Lime Kiln Theater**, an outdoor
venue in a 19th-century lime quarry · Boxerwood · Natural Bridge State Park · the Virginia Horse
Center.

> ⚠️ The Blue Ridge Parkway is **not** in Lexington — nearest access is 30–45 min out near
> Buchanan/Peaks of Otter. "Nearby," never "our Parkway." That line belongs to Waynesboro and Roanoke.

**Local words:** "the Institute" (VMI) · **"Keydets"** (never "Cadets") · "W&L" · "the Parade
Ground" · "the Chessie" · "Goshen" · "BV" (Buena Vista).

**Schools:** Rockbridge County HS — **Wildcats** (blue/silver/white) · Parry McCluer (BV) —
**Fighting Blues** · **VMI Keydets** (D-I, Southern Conf.) · **W&L Generals** (D-III, ODAC) ·
Southern Virginia **Knights**.

**Seasonal:** Foliage mid-to-late Oct, higher elevations early Oct. First frost Oct 16–31. Labor Day
is the informal end of Maury River tubing season. **Veterans Day is unusually significant here.**

**Dates Aug–Dec 2026:** Farmers market Weds through Nov 26 `[C26]` · **Rockbridge Regional Fair &

## ROANOKE — 2362 Peters Creek Rd Suite C
**Identity: Star City of the South**, and before that **the Magic City** — the N&W railroad took the
town from ~700 people to 5,000 in two years after 1882. Railroad origin story + outdoor town.
**Roanoke is not Shenandoah Valley** — it's "Virginia's Blue Ridge," its own region.

**Landmarks:** **the Mill Mountain Star** — first lit **Nov 23, 1949**, the largest freestanding
illuminated man-made star · **Hotel Roanoke** (1882 Tudor railroad hotel; its Christmas tree is a
real local tradition) · **the Historic City Market** — oldest continuously operating open-air market
in Virginia, dating to 1882, current building 1922; open year-round 8a–5p, closed only Christmas and
New Year's; heaviest Fri/Sat mornings; locals say "the Market" · Center in the Square · Taubman
Museum · O. Winston Link Museum (in the old N&W passenger station) · Virginia Museum of
Transportation · Roanoke River Greenway through Wasena, Vic Thomas and River's Edge · Elmwood Park ·
**Carvins Cove** — ~12,000 acres, 60+ miles of trail, **second-largest municipal park in the U.S.**;
locals say "the Cove" · **McAfee Knob** — 50,000+ hikers a year, **the most-photographed spot on the
entire Appalachian Trail**; with Tinker Cliffs and Dragon's Tooth it forms the **Virginia Triple
Crown** · Grandin Village and the **Grandin Theatre** (restored 1932 Art Deco nonprofit cinema) ·
Black Dog Salvage · Explore Park · Roanoke Mountain loop.
*Store-adjacent:* the Peters Creek / Williamson Rd / Hollins corridor, Green Ridge, Hollins University.

**Local words:** "the Star City" · "the Star" · **"Big Lick"** (the original colonial name, from the
salt licks that drew game — still used affectionately) · "the Cove" · "Grandin" · "the Market" ·
"the Valley" (meaning the Roanoke Valley: Roanoke, Salem, Vinton, the County).

**Schools:** Patrick Henry **Patriots** (purple/gold) · William Fleming **Colonels** (royal
blue/gold) — *PH vs Fleming is the crosstown rivalry* · Salem **Spartans** · Cave Spring **Knights**
· Hidden Valley **Titans** · Northside **Vikings** · Glenvar **Highlanders** · William Byrd
**Terriers** · Roanoke College **Maroons** · Hollins University · Virginia Tech **Hokies** (real
pull into the Valley; 2026 home: Sept 5 VMI, Sept 12 ODU, Fri Oct 2 Pitt, Oct 17 Homecoming, Nov 28
Commonwealth Clash).

**Seasonal:** Parkway foliage peaks mid-to-late Oct into early Nov. **Latest first frost of the
five, ~Oct 22.** Mildest winters, ~15" snow/yr.

**Dates Aug–Dec 2026:** Vinton Fall Festival Sat Oct 10 `[C26]` · **GO Fest Oct 16–18, now at
Elmwood Park** (moved from River's Edge) `[C26]` · **Drumstick Dash Thu Nov 26** `[C26]` · **Dickens
of a Christmas, 44th year — Fri Dec 4, Fri Dec 11, Fri Dec 18, 6–10p** `[C26]`, with **city tree
lighting Dec 4** (ceremony 5:30p, lights 6:20p, Salem Ave Plaza), **city Christmas parade Dec 11
6:30p** (Jefferson → Campbell → Williamson, rain or shine), **SPCA pet costume contest Dec 18** ·
Salem Christmas Parade Fri Dec 4 `[C26]` · **VHSL State Football Championships at Salem Stadium Dec
11–12** — 2026 is the first year Salem hosts Classes 3 & 4 too; Salem's pride in being Virginia's
high school football championship town is genuine `[C26]` · Illuminights at Explore Park `[VERIFY]`.

**Do not post as upcoming / defunct:** Big Lick Blues Festival (defunct, last confirmed 2011) ·
Roanoke Valley Horse Show (**relocated to the Virginia Horse Center in Lexington**) · "Fashionation"
is not a verified event name.

## How to extend this file
Lane C appends verified local detail here as it finds it, with a confidence tag and a date. Anything
that turns out wrong gets **struck and annotated, never silently deleted** — a wrong local fact that
gets quietly removed will be rediscovered and re-posted by a future run.

## Known gap — `com_local_news_desk` format (added 2026-08-24)
The drift engine's `com_local_news_desk` format asks for "something verifiable that happened here
this week" — genuine local news, sourced and dated. As of 2026-08-24 there is no live news-verification
step in the vp-community-weekly pipeline (no news search run before drafting). Runs so far have
substituted verified ongoing/seasonal facts already in this KB (market season dates, trail/park
status, drive-in season, etc.) framed as "worth knowing this week" rather than actual breaking news —
safe under Hard Rule 3, but not what the format was designed for. If a future session adds a real
local-news search step, note it here and remove this gap notice.
