# Academy edits from the November 2026 knowledge harvest

Proposed lesson changes from what calls, texts and staff questions showed. Apply in the next lesson pass; re-run the build + validator. Source: Knowledge Harvest/2026-11.

1. **L1-05 What you never say on the phone** — Drill the three-part check (full name, DOB, phone on file) before ANY account detail, and never confirm a firearm. Use real examples: first-name-only lookups, quoted payoffs, 'still in research'. Stop at 'come in with your ID'. (seen 260×; policy: CEO decision 10/25 (Account Details by Phone & Text); P&P 07.03)  _(evidence: C1, C2, C3, C5, C6, C31, C33, C34)_
2. **L3-06 The past-due call** — No grace period, hold, 'you'll be fine', '75-day policy' or 'five-day grace', in any form. Say only 'Your loan is due on its due date.' Never promise a hold; read dates and fees from Bravo. Fix text replies like 'yes ma'am' to a late-pay promise. (seen 111×; policy: 05.0, 06.01, 08.03; CEO decision 10/25)  _(evidence: C14, C15, C16, C18, C37, C38, C39, C40)_
3. **L6-03 The background check: approved, delayed, denied** — Add the rule that approval, delay and denial are not given by phone, text or voicemail, even to a returning customer. Teach the 'call or come in with ID' wording once Preston approves it. (seen 16×; policy: CEO decision 10/25; 04.02, 05.01)  _(evidence: C34, C61, C62, C63, C64, C66, C106, C130)_
4. **L7-03 The phone standard: quote it or capture it** — When we don't have the item: check the other four stores and take name and number. No quoting sight unseen. Praise the good examples: the stock-check and callback follow-through, and the loan-range call that explained limits. (seen 104×; policy: 07.03, 07.04; Market Manager 10/23 (no want-list))  _(evidence: C19, C21, C22, C23, C24, C25, C27, C28)_
5. **L6-04 Receiving an FFL transfer** — Every store receives transfers. Give the $25 fee and the right FFL link (thevalleypawn.com/ffl-transfer/). Never answer 'no' without checking. Say plainly that suppressor transfer paperwork is not handled, and answer private-transfer questions. (seen 6×; policy: 04.02 Person-to-Person Firearm Transfer; Store facts)  _(evidence: C65, C82, T115, T116, T118, T119, T120, T37)_
6. **L4-05 Is it really theirs? Ownership, red flags, police and the daily report** — Add a stolen-item inquiry segment: no confirm or deny, advise a police report, tell the Manager. Verify police callers (name, badge, department, call back via main number). Don't tell callers about building security. (seen 19×; policy: 07.03 police line (draft); 09.07; 01.06)  _(evidence: C49, C51, C52, C53, C54, C50, C72)_
7. **L7-07 When it goes wrong: service recovery and saying no well** — Before saying 'we don't take that', offer a loan and invite them in. Don't invent buying 'focus' or season reasons. Give one consistent answer on new-in-box items, TV age and phones. (seen 97×; policy: 04.05 Items We Don't Accept; queue: script for items we cannot take)  _(evidence: C20, C79, C84, C85, C87, C88, C89, C94)_
8. **L7-04 Texting, loyalty and Google reviews** — Add inbox discipline: reply to every thread, take a customer back from the auto-reply, and leave no follow-up unanswered. A review request must not follow a complaint thread. (seen 60×; policy: no (see text inbox candidate))  _(evidence: T1, T6, T7, T36, T46, T50, T62, T11)_
9. **L8-05 Calling and texting customers: the legal limits (annual recert)** — Honour STOP and unsubscribe requests. Tell callers confused by the app-link text that it is just a link to the app and does not mean they have a loan. (seen 3×; policy: 07.06 Telemarketing)  _(evidence: K76, K77, T138, T139)_
10. **L6-02 The 4473, section by section** — Add the three-letter ID code check, the handgun trade-in box, second-person review before release, and the $2 box once decided. Teach what a delayed check means for a redemption. (seen 8×; policy: 04.02, 05.02, 10.06 (steps pending approval))  _(evidence: K47, K48, K49, K59, K60, K10, S5, C151)_
11. **L4-03 Making the offer: research, quote, negotiate, get approval** — Add: never finalize before approval, call Preston on anything unusual, and send item photos and the loyalty report with every approval request. Metals: correct melt value first. (seen 4×; policy: no (queue: Manager transaction limits))  _(evidence: K9, K28, K56, K27)_
12. **L1-04 Your tools: Bravo, Slack, Chekkit, #ask-goldilocks** — Add: no personal calls or gossip on recorded store lines, and no card numbers read out on them. Questions go to #ask-goldilocks. (seen 5×; policy: 03.06 Telephone Usage)  _(evidence: C114, C156)_
