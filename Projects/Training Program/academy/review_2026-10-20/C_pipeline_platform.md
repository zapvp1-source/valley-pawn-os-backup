## 5. Verdict

**Launch after fixes — for Joshua's solo walkthrough; not ready for staff.**

The build pipeline itself is solid: 71/71 packages build deterministically, the SCORM 1.2 contract is implemented correctly and conservatively (verified by the node self-test and a live Chromium click-through at desktop and phone sizes with no console errors), audio embedding and autoplay handling are right for TalentLMS's frame, and the quizzes are structurally clean (one correct answer, never the longest, 80% mark, shuffled, retakes). Joshua can walk through it tomorrow.

Three things stop a staff launch. First, the citation leak that was declared fixed on 10/14 is not fully fixed: L7-08 Q2 shows learners "(CEO greeting script 2026-10-13; §04.01)" and L7-12 shows "9/28 huddle" — the filter's date and huddle patterns are too narrow, and the builder's own validator passes them (B1). Second, the builder has no guard that narration is present and no transcript when it is not (B3/B4) — the exact failure that produced silent intro packages on 10/14 can recur on any settings change, and a learner with sound off loses rule content that exists only in narration. Third, and structurally: the portal is on the Free plan, so the program's defining mechanics — ordered level unlocking, 20+ learners, the Monday scorecard, the manager nudge, Gusto sync — have no system behind them today, and there is no written manual fallback for enrolling one level per week by hand. Everything in sections 2a–2c is a few hours of code-and-free-JSON edits and costs almost no ElevenLabs characters; section 2d needs Joshua's purchase decision and a one-page enrolment runbook before the first employee is invited.
