## The 10 policy candidates (next Policy Day)

| # | Candidate | Seen | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One answer customers hear on late loans; holds; reopening an expired loan | 92× | **Yes, settled today**: due on its due date, late fee day 6, hold through day 30, pull day 31. Holds and reopening: CEO question 2 |
| 2 | Firearm returns, "won't fire" complaints, denied trades | 9× | **Adjust**: the P&P already covers it (no gun returns; Store Manager and Market Manager decide within 30 days; a denied person never gets a gun back). **Also in the harvest (not a decision, a compliance risk):** a working note describes writing part of a large loan, redeeming it the next day with fees waived, then rewriting it. The P&P (Section 05.0) forbids dividing loans and waiving or adding charges. The Market Manager should stop this now.
