## What moved and why

**Email is the real story.** After a month of the weekly newsletter effectively not sending to its real list, the W-series resumed and immediately produced the two best-performing sends on record. Store Spotlight is now a confirmed-twice winning format. The one soft spot: primary-CTA clicks have been zero for three sends running — worth a creative look, but not urgent given calls+texts is the metric that actually drives store visits.

**eBay is quietly shrinking while getting more efficient.** Sell-through improved (34% → 37%) and the aged-inventory dollar figure came down slightly, but the active listing count keeps dropping week over week. A store isn't goosing efficiency by clearing inventory faster than it lists — it's running out of things to sell. This is exactly what the open EBAY-FLOOR action item is about.

**The noindex conflict from two weeks ago is resolved.** Both the website-health lane and the presence-audit lane now independently confirm the 5 location pages are indexed and have stayed that way. Closed in the action register.

**A new, small NAP drift surfaced:** Harrisonburg's Google Business Profile still carries "Ste 22" in the address, which was removed from the website and schema on 8/23. Two different systems (our site and Google's listing) now disagree about our own address — a five-minute Google Business Profile edit fixes it.
