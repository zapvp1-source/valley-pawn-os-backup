03 — Cross-Channel PatternsFive failures that repeat in every channel.

These are not six separate marketing problems. They are five problems appearing six times.

Pattern 01 · Highest impact

Volume is being optimised. Targeting is not.

Every channel is tuned to produce more, and every channel's own data says precision beats volume by one to two orders of magnitude.

EMAIL 11,245-person blast → 16 call/text clicks (1.4 per 1,000) 144-person store segment → 36 call/text clicks (250 per 1,000) ← 176× better SOCIAL 106 gold-buying posts → median reach 4 6 hiring posts → 16.7% of ALL reach, 39% of ALL clicks EBAY Culpeper: 307 listings → $82 revenue per listing Lexington: 27 listings → $433 revenue per listing ← 5.3× better

The standing social plan targets 55 posts/week by December, up from 17. The bottom 300 posts of the last quarter produced 10.6% of total reach. Tripling output at the current median adds roughly 340 reach per week. Fixing targeting is worth an order of magnitude more and costs less.

Pattern 02

A large share of output lands on broken plumbing.

Not weak content — content delivered to an address that cannot receive it.

  • Harrisonburg posted all quarter to Facebook page 474248069342834 — the page your own build spec labels "legacy… do not post to it." It also has the highest median reach of any store page (46). Best page, wrong target.
  • Roanoke published 81 posts into a profile with ~35 followers — 15% of network output for 3.8% of reach. The setup doc to fix it exists and was never run.
  • SPF is a hard PermError on fcfpawn.com — two v=spf1 records at the apex, neither including Brevo. SPF does not pass at all. thevalleypawn.com was never authenticated.
  • JSON-LD does not parse on all five sell-gold-{city} pages — "dayOfWeek": "["Monday",…]". Google discards the entire LocalBusiness + FAQ block on your five highest-intent commercial pages.
  • Meta Page tokens are dead (since 4 Jul, re-killed 21 Aug). eBay API returns 403 on Marketing/Analytics/Seller Standards for all 5 stores, unchanged since June.
Pattern 03

Everything high-intent is unmeasured; everything measured is low-intent.

Google Business Profile is where "pawn shop near me" lands — the surface closest to a walk-in. Publer returns api_total: 0 for all five GBP accounts, and GBP admin sits on a Google account nobody can reach programmatically. Facebook — 82 link clicks in 90 days — is the fully instrumented one.

On the website, phone_click and directions_click fire. sms_click does not, despite text being a stated primary goal. There is no form to submit anywhere on the site, so form_submit has nothing to track.

Pattern 04

The finished-but-never-shipped pile is the cheapest money in the business.

Work that is fully built, fully specified, and idle:

Forfeited-loan win-back .... strategy + copy done 15 Jun · list + 4 attributes built 10 Jul · 0 contacts, 10 weeks vp-ad-engine ............... 470-line pipeline, 11 batch runs, 0 ad packs ever produced Sunset/dormant sweep ....... sequence ran to completion 6 Jul · the cull itself never executed directory-listing-monitor .. snapshot path points at a dead session dir; never produced an artifact Review schema .............. specced in STRATEGY.md §6 · 0 aggregateRating occurrences sitewide eBay 90-day pull gate ...... policy written 4 Jul · sent to Gusto with ZERO recipients Tracking plan .............. 11 events, 8 dimensions, GTM container — 2 events shipped

The forfeited-loan list is the warmest audience in pawn: people who already borrowed against goods at your counter and lost them. It is one Bravo report away from live, and has been for ten weeks.

Pattern 05

Your single best asset is being generated and then discarded.

Four stores generated 21 new Google reviews in one week. That is excellent for a 5-store chain and it is the strongest local-search signal you own. It appears nowhere: zero aggregateRating markup sitewide, zero embedded reviews on any location or money page — while the homepage claims "Average 4.9 Stars Across All Locations" with no count, no source, and no markup. To Google and to AI answer engines that reads as an unsubstantiated claim.

Worse, the auto-responder is filtered to 4–5★ only. That is the mechanical reason two 1-star Culpeper reviews alleging gold lowballing sat unanswered for two weeks — in the market where you otherwise dominate, while the brand ran gold-buy content. Culpeper also produced 0 review requests the week the other four produced 21.

  • Is eBay profitable? Unanswerable. No cost basis exists in any eBay dataset; fvf is null on all 555 sold records. Nothing joins an eBay item to its Bravo cost. Blocked on the SoldComps API key.

The only structured opt-in in the account is the $100 giveaway — 82 contacts. A double-opt-in template exists and no signup flow uses it. Resolve before the next full-list send.
