## Jurisdiction rules (verified Oct 2026)
- City of St. Augustine: STR allowed in RS-2 / RG-1 etc. with city registration; single-family zones = one turnover/week; HP districts = one/month.
- St. Augustine Beach: residential transient licenses capped at 100 and all in use → SFR only with transferable license (6 Lisbon). Condos/TH east of A1A Beach Blvd rent freely.
- Unincorporated St. Johns east of ICW: county vacation-rental registration (BTR + DBPR + DOR), 2 guests/bedroom max 10, 1 parking/3 guests.
- Orlando: City of Orlando bans whole-home STR; only Osceola/Polk STR-zoned resorts. Osceola license $250 + $160 inspection, $1M liability, 13.5% tax.
- Walton/30A: county STR certificate $300/yr required BEFORE advertising, $500/day fines, 11% tax, 4–8 weeks to compliance. No-STR communities excluded (Watersound Origins, NatureWalk, Seabreeze).
