### 01.08 Monthly Bonus Program
_Generated from bonus_rules.json on 2026-09-06 — effective for earning months from 2026-08. Supersedes all earlier versions of this section, including the 2.5%/50% and $3,000-minimum language carried over from the legacy manual._

Store employees earn a monthly incentive bonus, paid once a month on the Friday after the 15th.

- **Store revenue goal (hard gate).** If the store does not hit its monthly revenue goal, no bonus is
  paid to anyone at that store for that month.
- **Manager bonus:** 2.5% of store revenue when the store hits its revenue goal and all task goals;
  1.5% when it hits the revenue goal but misses a task goal.
- **Sales & Loan Associate / Representative bonus:** 5% of the gross profit on their own sales when
  the store hits its revenue goal and all task goals; 3% when it hits the revenue goal but misses a
  task goal.
- **Task goals:** Google reviews at least **15 new reviews** for the store that month; Email capture at least **50%** of customers leave an email address; Gold scrap at least **100 dwt** collected, in buckets closed during the month; Facebook followers at least **+15 net new followers** on the store's page that month; Beat last year the store's revenue for the month must be above the same month last year.
- **Store of the Month:** $300 split among the employees of the store winning the most
  categories, tie broken by gold weight.
- **Eligibility:** a manager must have managed the branch for the full month; all employees must be
  employed at the time of payout, not merely when the bonus was earned. Bonus pay is subject to
  supervisor discretion and may be reduced or changed, including for delinquencies beyond standard or
  breach of company policy. The company may modify the program at any time.
