### 01.08 Monthly Bonus Program
_Generated from bonus_rules.json on 2026-10-24 — effective for earning months from 2026-11. Supersedes all earlier versions of this section, including the 2.5%/50% and $3,000-minimum language carried over from the legacy manual._

Store employees earn a monthly incentive bonus, paid once a month on the Friday after the 15th of
the month following the month in which it was earned. When the 15th falls on a Friday, the bonus is
paid the following Friday.

- **Store revenue goal (hard gate).** If the store does not hit its monthly revenue goal, no bonus is
  paid to anyone at that store for that month.
- **Manager bonus:** 2.5% of store revenue when the store hits its revenue goal and all task goals;
  2% when it hits the revenue goal but misses a task goal.
- **Sales & Loan Associate / Representative bonus:** 5% of the gross profit on their own sales when
  the store hits its revenue goal and all task goals; 4% when it hits the revenue goal but misses a
  task goal.
- **Task goals:** Google reviews at least **17 new reviews** for the store that month; Email capture at least **60%** of customers leave an email address; Gold scrap at least **110 dwt** collected, in buckets closed during the month; Facebook followers at least **+5 net new followers** on the store's page that month; Beat last year the store's revenue for the month must be above the same month last year.
- **Eligibility:** a manager must have managed the branch for the full month; all employees must be
  employed at the time of payout, not merely when the bonus was earned.
- **Standard KPIs (from the December 2026 bonus month):** A bonus is earned only if the store meets every standard KPI in force on the 1st of the bonus month, in addition to the published revenue and task goals. Standard KPIs are every measurable store standard published in the Policies & Procedures Manual, the Employee Handbook, the store scorecard, or a signed Monthly Policy Update, as in force on the 1st of the bonus month — including NCO (Neat, Clean, Organized, Professional) checklist, drawer balancing and cash variances, monthly gun audit — every firearm accounted for, Monthly Store Compliance Checklist (VP-CP-07) by the 15th, nightly police report, loan delinquency and the default/pull timeline, the published bonus revenue and task goals. If the miss is caused by one employee's own documented policy violation, only that employee's bonus for that month is affected.
- **Approval, suspension and payment:** Every bonus is approved by management (CEO) before payroll. Approval confirms the published conditions were met; it is not a second, unpublished test. Management may suspend, change or end the bonus program for any store, any position or the whole company, for a legitimate business reason, by written notice. A suspension or change applies to bonus months that begin after the notice date (or a later date stated in the notice) and stays in place until lifted in writing. Once a bonus is earned for a completed month it is paid on the published payday. The Company does not reduce or withhold an earned bonus after the fact. Bonuses are nondiscretionary for wage-and-hour purposes; for hourly (nonexempt) employees they are included in the regular rate for overtime. This program is not a contract and does not change at-will employment.
