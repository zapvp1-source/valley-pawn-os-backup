## How you earn

Every month each store gets a **revenue goal**. Your bonus starts with that goal.

**1. The store has to hit its monthly revenue goal.** If the store misses its goal, there is no bonus
that month for anyone at that store — no partial, no exception. If the store hits it, everyone at
that store is in the money.

**2. Then the store's task goals decide your rate.** Hit all of them and your rate goes up; miss any
one of them and your rate goes down. Same swing either way.

| | Store hit its revenue goal **and** all task goals | Store hit its revenue goal, **missed** a task goal | Store missed its revenue goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manager** | 2.5% of store revenue | 2% of store revenue | $0 |
| **Sales & Loan Associate / Representative** | 5% of the gross profit on your own sales | 4% of the gross profit on your own sales | $0 |

## The task goals

- **Google reviews** — at least **17 new reviews** for the store that month. Ask for the review, use the signs.
- **Email capture** — at least **60%** of customers leave an email address. Ask for the email every time.
- **Gold scrap** — at least **110 dwt** collected, in buckets closed during the month. Ask for the gold.
- **Facebook followers** — at least **+5 net new followers** on the store's page that month. Ask for the follow, use the signs.
- **Beat last year** — the store's revenue for the month must be above the same month last year.

## The rules that decide who gets paid

- A manager must have run the store for the **full month** to be paid on the manager rate.
- You must be **employed on the day the bonus is paid**, not just the month you earned it.
- Bonus is **paid monthly, on the Friday after the 15th** of the following month.
- **Store standards (from the December 2026 bonus month).** A bonus is earned only if the store meets every standard KPI in force on the 1st of the bonus month, in addition to the published revenue and task goals. Standard KPIs means: every measurable store standard published in the Policies & Procedures Manual, the Employee Handbook, the store scorecard, or a signed Monthly Policy Update, as in force on the 1st of the bonus month. That includes NCO (Neat, Clean, Organized, Professional) checklist, drawer balancing and cash variances, monthly gun audit — every firearm accounted for, Monthly Store Compliance Checklist (VP-CP-07) by the 15th, nightly police report, loan delinquency and the default/pull timeline, the published bonus revenue and task goals.
- If the miss is caused by one employee's own documented policy violation, only that employee's bonus for that month is affected.
- **Management approval.** Every bonus is approved by management (CEO) before payroll. Approval confirms the published conditions were met; it is not a second, unpublished test.
- **Suspension.** Management may suspend, change or end the bonus program for any store, any position or the whole company, for a legitimate business reason, by written notice. A suspension or change applies to bonus months that begin after the notice date (or a later date stated in the notice) and stays in place until lifted in writing.
- **Earned means paid.** Once a bonus is earned for a completed month it is paid on the published payday. The Company does not reduce or withhold an earned bonus after the fact.
- Bonuses are nondiscretionary for wage-and-hour purposes; for hourly (nonexempt) employees they are included in the regular rate for overtime.
- This program is not a contract and does not change at-will employment.

## Where the numbers come from

Revenue is Bravo's Net Revenue for the store — pawn service charges (interest and fees) plus the
gross profit on sales. Gross profit on your own sales comes from your Bravo employee activity.
Reviews come from Chekkit, gold from the scrap buckets closed that month, emails from the customer
records entered at the counter, Facebook from Publer. Every number is pulled from those systems the
same way every month; nobody types a figure in by hand.
