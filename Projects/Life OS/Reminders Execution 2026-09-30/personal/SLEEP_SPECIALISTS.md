## What to ask for (from the case file, not a diagnosis)

These facts come from `Health Optimization/Master_Findings_Index.md`:

- Night episodes come **out of REM**, 2–3 times a night: hot feet, then hot body, then pain. Heart rate stays mostly flat. There are fasciculations, and new calf cramps during or just after REM (7/2026).
- Polysomnogram 1/2025 showed **mild OSA that was REM-predominant** (REM AHI 17.3 vs overall 6.2) plus **periodic limb movements**. A repeat study with the oral appliance in showed apnea acceptably controlled, but the REM episodes continue.
- So the question for a specialist is **why REM sleep triggers these events when the apnea is treated**. That calls for a sleep physician who is also a **neurologist** (parasomnia, limb movements, autonomic arousal). A pulmonary or apnea-only practice is the wrong fit.

**When booking, ask for:** a new-patient visit with a neurologist sleep specialist for REM-related nocturnal events and PLMS with OSA already treated. Ask whether a repeat in-lab **video polysomnogram with extended EEG/EMG leads** is appropriate. Bring the 1/2025 PSG report, the with-appliance repeat study, and an Oura hypnogram export of several episode nights (`Nights_Ledger.csv` / `Episode_Log.csv`).
