1 · What happens to first-payment defaults

Of the 298 loans that hit their pull date with no payment ever made (first-payment defaults, loan snapshots 7/22–9/6 plus one Culpeper list from 5/18), 140 (47%) were forfeited and 86 (29%) were cured — paid, redeemed or renewed. The other 72 (24%) can't be called yet: no loan snapshot exists after 9/6 (11 of them were confirmed still on loan and unpaid on 9/30). Once a loan reaches its pull date unpaid, about 62% of the ones that resolve are lost. Small loans are the worst: 85% of resolved $50–99 loans forfeited vs 33% of $500+ loans. Looking back two years the other way: 70% of all loans forfeited since July 2025 (2,129 loans, $299,328) never had a single paid extension — first-payment defaults are most of what the company forfeits.

2 · Where our jewelry comes from

From 7/16/2025 to 7/15/2026 the five stores took in $707,079 of jewelry at cost: $554,342 (78%) from counter buys (5,620 items) and $152,737 (22%) from forfeited loans (1,081 items). Leaving scrap out (it goes to the refiner, not the case), it is still 73% buys / 27% forfeits. Roanoke leans most on forfeits (31% of its jewelry $); Lexington the least (11%). Jewelry is 46% of every dollar the stores spend buying at the counter.

3 · Cancelled layaways

Last 12 closed months (Sep 2025 – Aug 2026): 195 layaways cancelled out of 1,350 opened (14%), worth $117,122 in sales that didn't happen; customers had paid $27,051 in before cancelling. Another 94 expired ($11,753 paid in). Forfeited deposits since Feb 2025: $0 — expired-layaway money now goes to the customer as layaway store credit (none of that credit has ever expired); the only forfeitures on record are $731 kept as restocking fees in Dec 2024 – Jan 2025, before the store-credit rule. Trend: cancellations spiked in Mar – Apr 2026 (48 layaways, $44,484), stayed high through July (19, 20, 25 in May–July) and fell to 12 in August, against roughly 13 a month in Dec 2024 – Aug 2025. Roanoke has not expired a single layaway since March 2025 — it cancels them instead (54 cancellations in 12 months).

1 · First-payment defaults: forfeited vs cured

A first-payment default here is a loan that showed up on Bravo's "Claude First Payment Default" list — on loan, past its pull date (due date + 16 days), and no payment ever made. Each one is then followed to 9/29: Forfeited = it's in Bravo's expired-loan list; Cured = it dropped off a later list without expiring (a payment, redemption or renewal — the data can't say which); Unresolved in data = still on the store's last list and not expired by 9/29. The forfeit rate is taken over resolved loans only. This is follow-through only — the weekly #first-payment-default report is not repeated here.

What happened to the customers' money

  • Expired layaways: since Feb 2025 every dollar paid in goes to the customer as layaway store credit ($11,753 in the last 12 months). $3,440 of layaway credit was paid out or used; $0 of layaway credit has expired in any month on file. That matches the P&P rule (expiry = store credit, not forfeiture).
  • Before Feb 2025: expired-layaway money was kept as a restocking fee — $731 in Dec 2024 – Jan 2025. That is the only forfeited layaway money in the records.
  • Cancelled layaways: $27,051 paid in over 12 months left the layaway account with no restocking fee kept. The End of Month report does not show whether it went back as cash or as general store credit (the P&P says a customer who changes their mind gets store credit).
  • Roanoke: 54 cancellations and zero expirations in 12 months (its last expiry was March 2025) — Roanoke appears to close lapsed layaways by cancelling them instead of expiring them. If so, those customers don't go through the layaway-credit path the other four stores use. Worth one question to Roanoke; nothing was changed.

Data used, date ranges & limits

  • First-payment defaults — thin coverage, read with care. The loan lists only exist for these dates: Culpeper 05/18, 07/22, 09/06; Harrisonburg 07/22, 08/03, 08/10, 08/23, 08/30, 09/06; Lexington 07/22, 08/03, 08/10, 08/23, 08/30, 09/06; Roanoke 07/22, 08/03, 08/10, 08/23, 09/06; Waynesboro 07/22, 08/03, 08/10, 08/23, 09/06. Culpeper has only 3 lists; Roanoke and Waynesboro have no 8/30 list (their "8/30" files are really 9/6 copies, so capture dates were read from each loan, not the filename). The 5/18 lists for the other four stores saved only a count, not the loans. No list exists after 9/6, so 72 loans (24%) can't be called. "Cured" includes any payment, redemption, renewal or void — a loan that got one payment and then expired later still counts as Forfeited. Expired-loan list: Bravo "Claude Forfeiture Winback", all 5 stores, expirations 11/15/2024 – 9/29/2026 (loans pawned from ~9/30/2024).
  • Reverse view: "never paid once" = due date never moved past pawn + 30 days. Limited to expirations since 7/1/2025 because the expired-loan list starts with loans pawned ~9/30/2024, which would otherwise over-count never-paid loans.
  • Category fix from the earlier run: the first version filed Chainsaws as jewelry and coins/bullion as jewelry. Categories are now mapped by exact Bravo name; coins & bullion are their own group.
  • Jewelry refreshed 9/30/2026. The only report that splits both buys and forfeits by jewelry category is Bravo's Pawn Activity Summary. It was re-pulled for all 5 stores on 9/30 for 7/16/2025 – 7/15/2026 (7/15/2026 is the latest complete day Bravo will run it for). The "since the last pull" block is the new pull minus the 7/15 pull, per store and category. Forfeits are gross: Bravo shows reactivated loans (customer came back after expiry) only as a store total — 584 loans / $89,310 company-wide that year, all merchandise — so a small part of the forfeited jewelry went back to its owner.
  • Layaways: End of Month reports Dec 2024 – Aug 2026, all 5 stores, every month present (105 store-months). September 2026 isn't closed yet. Bravo's own layaway block didn't tie to the dollar in 6 store-months (gaps of $20–$450 in deposits, likely a void or adjustment); the cancellation lines were still read straight from the report: ROA 2026-05; ROA 2026-06; CUL 2026-07; LEX 2026-07; ROA 2026-07; WAY 2026-07.
  • Nothing was posted to Slack and no one was messaged. Scripts: fpd_outcomes.py, jewelry_sourcing.py, jewelry_sourcing_refresh.py, layaway_cancellations.py, build_report_refresh.py. The only Bravo contact was the standard report pull through the existing export queue.
