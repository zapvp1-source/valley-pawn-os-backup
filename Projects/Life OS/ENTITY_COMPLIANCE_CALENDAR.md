## Recurring deadlines

### Virginia — LLC annual registration fee

**$50 per LLC, due the last day of the LLC's anniversary month** (the month it was approved).
No annual *report* is required for VA LLCs — it is a fee only. **$25 late penalty. If unpaid by the
last day of the 3rd month after the due date, the LLC is automatically cancelled by operation of
law.** Pay online through the SCC Clerk's Information System (cis.scc.virginia.gov).

### Tennessee — annual report (Farming Infinity Tennessee LLC)

Required of **foreign LLCs holding a Certificate of Authority**, not just domestic ones.

- **Due: April 1** each year (1st day of the 4th month after fiscal year end; calendar-year entity).
- **Fee: $300 minimum** ($50/member, minimum $300, maximum $3,000). Single-member → $300.
- **60-day grace period**, then administrative dissolution and revocation of the Certificate of
  Authority — which would also knock out the ability to bring an eviction on Hardinberry.
- **First report due: April 1, 2027.**
