# Client Digital Footprint Intake Form

Fill this out for every new client before starting any build work. Modeled on the Solaterra LLC
contact record — the goal is to leave this session (or the next one) with everything needed to
build the site, claim/optimize Google Business Profile, sync directories, and link socials without
having to go back and ask the owner five more times.

Save the completed copy as: `Documents/Claude/Projects/Client Sites/<Client Name>/CONTACT.md`
