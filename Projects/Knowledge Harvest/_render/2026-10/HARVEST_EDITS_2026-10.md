# Academy edits from the October 2026 knowledge harvest

Proposed lesson changes from what calls, texts and staff questions showed. Apply in the next lesson pass; re-run the build + validator. Source: Knowledge Harvest/2026-10.

1. **L1-05 What you never say on the phone** — Drill identity verification before any loan, layaway, collateral or firearm detail. Use the account holder, not a shared number or a ticket number, and give no detail to unverified 'police' callers. Praise the staff who refused. (seen 59×; policy: 03.09 Confidentiality; 09.07; queued 'Account Details by Phone & Text')  _(evidence: C1, C19, C20, C21, C22, C23, C24, C41)_
2. **M-01 Manager track: the weekly layaway review and the loan timeline** — Retire the 75-day and five-day-grace lines. Stop verbal and note-based holds past default. Teach the post-Oct 1 timeline and who approves exceptions, starting with Roanoke. (seen 85×; policy: 06.01 (also 05.01, 08.03))  _(evidence: C7, C8, C10, C12, C21, C44, C45, C48)_
3. **L6-05 Firearm pawns, redemptions and holds** — Add scenarios: no third-party pawn of a gun without the owner and valid ID, no background-check results by phone, a one-cent balance still needs the transfer, sale then layaway. (seen 8×; policy: 05.01 Firearm redemption; 04.02)  _(evidence: C72, C71, K97, K98, C18, C16)_
4. **L6-02 The 4473, section by section** — One age and sale standard, with no 'case by case based on who you are'. Teach the answer to customers who cite new ATF rulings: refer to management and make no promises. (seen 2×; policy: 05.02 age note; 12.05 under-21 rule)  _(evidence: C67, C70)_
5. **L6-06 “My gun won't fire”: the after-sale call** — Staff gave DIY advice (oil the chamber) and discussed trades. Teach: no troubleshooting advice, escalate to the manager, and any firearm return or trade needs FFL paperwork. Include the no-return answer for guns. (seen 4×; policy: 04.02; 05.02; 05.08)  _(evidence: C32, C34, C68, C93)_
6. **L7-03 The phone standard: quote it or capture it** — Holds over a couple of minutes lose sales (20 long-hold flags). Teach return-to-line checks, capturing the callback, and not guaranteeing a price (K74). New hires without system access should hand off, not guess. (seen 22×; policy: 07.03 Telephone Etiquette)  _(evidence: C49, C55, C56, C57, C60, C74, C71, K74)_
7. **L7-07 When it goes wrong: service recovery and saying no well** — Teach: never name competitors or Marketplace, and say 'we are unable to process new items right now' without reasons. Check other Valley Pawn stores instead of sending customers away. (seen 6×; policy: no (Preston rule K35/K91 not yet in the manual))  _(evidence: K35, K91, C25, C27, C77, C79)_
8. **L7-02 Free layaway and the 30-day warranty** — Teach the three layaway tiers, the monthly payment, the 60-day expiry that issues store credit, and cancellation in store only. One store quoted '90 days' for a $700 item. (seen 4×; policy: 05.05 Layaway)  _(evidence: C43, K67, K68, K69)_
9. **L1-05 What you never say on the phone (hours and made-up policy)** — Add a store-by-store hours drill. Saturday is 5 PM at Culpeper, Roanoke and Waynesboro. Roanoke has been open Wednesdays since 9/30 and Waynesboro since 10/14. Never say 'check the manual' if unsure; ask. (seen 26×; policy: 01.01 Hours; Store facts)  _(evidence: C46, C43, C45, T157, T1)_
10. **L1-04 Your tools: Bravo, Slack, Chekkit, #ask-goldilocks** — Teach: hang up after leaving a voicemail (two lines stayed open and recorded in-store conversations), set up voicemail boxes, and use backup card readers and escalation for system trouble. (seen 6×; policy: 03.06 Telephone Usage; 03.09)  _(evidence: C119, C134, C160, C161, C163, C128, K59, K60)_
11. **L4-05 Is it really theirs? Ownership, red flags, police and the daily report** — Add: ask casually who the clear-out company is, never split items to recover cost, and handle lost-ID sellers. Teach a consistent customer script that all intake goes to police daily. (seen 8×; policy: 05.00A Required Transaction Record; 09.07)  _(evidence: K62, K63, K64, K86, C50, C38, C40, C39)_
12. **new Spotting phishing and spam to store email** — Add a short lesson on fake 'reminder / profile update' emails, vendor spam and unknown senders: no clicking, no replies, and report to Preston. (seen 8×; policy: 03.17 Store Email Password Policy; 03.07)  _(evidence: E28, E29, E30, E31, E32, E34, E36)_
13. **L8-02 Watches and high-end brands** — Add Preston's rules: open the case back and check the movement, ask him before any higher-end brand, and no resale of a counterfeit even labelled replica without a written decision. (seen 5×; policy: no)  _(evidence: K87, K88, K89, C36, C54)_
