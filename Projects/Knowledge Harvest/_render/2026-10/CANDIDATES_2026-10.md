3. **What will you give me for my item (text photos)?** (text, 20×) — Please text clear photos, brand/model, condition, what comes with it and whether you want to sell or pawn. Any offer is an estimate until we inspect and test it in store. — issue: Many photo texts got no human reply.  _(evidence: A5, A10, T7, T13, T23, T4, T8, T20)_
4. **What kinds of items do you take?** (text, 10×) — We mainly look for up-to-date electronics, gold, silver, firearms, guitars and other items that sell quickly. Send a photo and we'll tell you. — issue: Answers about phones, TVs and tools differ by store.  _(evidence: T109, C97, C82, T55, T156)_
5. **Can my spouse or a friend get information about my loan or pick up my item?** (phone, 5×) — We can only discuss an account with the account holder. Please have the account holder call, or come in with ID. — issue: Several unverified third parties were given details.  _(evidence: C15, C16, C78, C20, C61, C65)_
6. **Did my stolen item get pawned at your store?** (phone, 6×) — We can't confirm or deny who sells to us. Please file a police report. We report every pawn and purchase to police daily, so they can check. — issue: One employee searched a name for an outside caller. Replies said 'every night' or 'end of every shift'; the manual says noon next day.  _(evidence: C39, C40, C42, C28, C38, C37)_
7. **How do I get my pawned gun back?** (phone, 4×) — Getting a pawned gun back is a transfer. You fill out the federal paperwork again and a state background check is run, so the owner must come in with valid ID. — issue: One employee said a spouse could pawn it without the owner's ID.  _(evidence: C18, C16, T116, C72)_
8. **How does a pawn loan work and what will it cost?** (phone, 67×) — You bring an item of value and we loan against it. The pawn ticket shows the amount borrowed, the fees and the total to redeem, and we go through it with you before you sign. There is no credit check and no collection if you don't repay.  _(evidence: C18, C13)_
9. **When does my loan default and is there a grace period?** (phone, 85×) — _no good answer seen_ — issue: Answers conflict: 75 days, five-day grace, 15 days, and promised holds. Awaiting Preston/a team member's customer wording.  _(evidence: C44, C45, C48, C21, C8)_
10. **How does layaway work?** (phone, 25×) — Layaway is free with no interest or fees. Jewelry takes 10% down over up to 10 months. Other items under $500 take 20% down over up to 3 months.
