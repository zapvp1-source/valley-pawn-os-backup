## Held pending answers (NOT changed)

Code § 54.1-4009(C)(2) allows a late fee only if the customer was told about it on the pawn ticket. The v2026.9 sentences ("lawful ONLY if the customer was notified of it on the pawn ticket. Confirm the fee appears on the printed ticket before it is charged" and "only where that fee was disclosed on the ticket") are kept word for word. Only the amount (5% of principal) and the 5-day grace were changed. This needs Preston's answer and a legal check before Policy Day. Until then the Academy (lessons say it is not on the ticket and staff say it out loud) and the P&P disagree.
2. **Extension and renewal definitions (05.04, glossary).** The draft only adds what ruling 19 says: they are separate options, a reloan needs a new worksheet and a renewal does not, renewals show the correct loan date in Bravo, and there is no redemption ticket. These are unchanged: the 05.04 "Refinance" heading and its Bravo "Renewal" steps, the "PAYMENTS AND PRINCIPAL" paragraph ("the refinance (the extension)", "the app takes refinance (extension) payments only"), the glossary entry "Refinance", and the "always offer the refinance option" line. They need Preston's one-line definition of each option. Also unconfirmed: whether "a new worksheet" means the Large Loan/Buy Worksheet (01.10, $1,000+) or a new loan write-up.
