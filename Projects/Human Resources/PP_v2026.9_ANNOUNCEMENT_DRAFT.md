**Updated P&P Manual (v2026.9) and Employee Handbook (v2026.8) — effective October 21, 2026**

We've updated both manuals so they match what the Valley Pawn Academy teaches. What changed:

• **Store hours:** Culpeper, Roanoke and Waynesboro are open Mon–Sat, 10–6 weekdays and **10–5 on Saturday**. Harrisonburg and Lexington are open 10–6 and closed Wednesday. Every store is closed Sunday. Be in by 9:30.
• **Greeting:** "Welcome to Valley Pawn! Have you ever been here before?" First-timers hear the three things: 30-day warranty, free layaway and the app. Before anyone leaves, they've also heard what's on clearance.
• **Account details** go only to the account holder: their name plus the ticket number, date of birth or last four of the phone on file. That includes spouses: they come in with ID and the ticket.
• **Police on the phone:** take their name and department and call back on the department's main number. **Background checks by phone:** "Your background check is finished, you can come in." Never say approved or denied.
• **Discounts:** up to 30% is yours. 31–50% needs the Store Manager on the ticket. Never more than 50%.
• **Warranty** needs an account with an email. **Returns** need the receipt and original condition. No firearm returns.
• **Counterfeits:** check every $50 and $100 with the UV light and watermark. Never hand a suspect bill back; it goes to the Store Manager.
• **Past-due loans:** Day 1 past due is the day after the due date. A first-payment default is marked expired right away, but nothing is pulled or sold before the 15-day grace period ends.
• **Firearms:** nobody under 21 buys a handgun or an assault firearm. A real gift paid for with the buyer's own money is fine. If someone else picked it, is paying or will pay them back, stop.

You'll get both manuals in Gusto to sign. Questions go to your Store Manager or Preston.
