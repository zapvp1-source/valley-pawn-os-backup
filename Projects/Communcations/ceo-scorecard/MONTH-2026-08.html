The trend

  • Growth is broad and it is holding — August +32.9% YoY, year to date +37.4%, trailing twelve months +32.3%, and every one of the five stores grew at least 23%.
  • It is being bought, not priced: the loan book is up 21.7% and inventory 35.5% year over year, and total assets set a record $1,452K in August.
  • Roanoke is now both the fastest grower (+46.6%) and the worst-aged book in the company. Those two facts are related.

Financial
