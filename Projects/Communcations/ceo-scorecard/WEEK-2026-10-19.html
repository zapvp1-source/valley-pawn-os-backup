Revenue & Sales

  • Net revenue $46,731, up 9.3% on last week and 42.3% on last year; gold & silver sales were just $3,895 with no refinery settlement, so the growth is loan fees and retail, not metals.
  • Culpeper led the week again ($11,907, +0.3% WoW, 86.8% to its October target); Roanoke surged 53.9% WoW to $11,331 (+134.6% YoY) on a 69% jump in loan fees.
  • Waynesboro fell 24.3% WoW to $8,730 after a $13,843 prior week — the company's sharpest week-over-week drop.

Loans

  • 128 loans forfeited company-wide ($19,398), more than double last week's 58 ($6,995); Harrisonburg alone forfeited 94 ($14,560).
  • First-payment defaults improved sharply: 159 loans / $16,745 exposure, down from 209 / $24,197 — Harrisonburg went from the company's worst (84 loans) to its best (12).
  • New loans written $27,167 (170 loans), down 25.2% WoW; loan balance $734,431, down 1.4% on the week.

Operations

  • Aged 1yr+ inventory $106,222 (14.94%), down slightly from 15.15%; Roanoke worst at 26.96% ($34,217), Waynesboro cleanest at 6.20%.
  • Markdown backlog jumped to 175 items / $36,578 from 115 / $25,724 last week — mostly jewelry (105 items / $34,065) — building, not shrinking.
  • #emails-missed has posted nothing since Sep 11 — six weeks dark; that's a reporting gap, not a clean week.

People

  • Roanoke lost its second hourly associate (Jacob Cox, separated Oct 20) and ran the back half of the week on one person, 9.9 overtime hours; Brendon Thomas starts today (Oct 26).
  • Lexington ran the whole week on one person (Uriah Tiglao) again — 4.5 overtime hours, no breaks logged on any of his 5 shifts, a recurring pattern.
  • 18 active employees; Harrisonburg and Waynesboro are furthest behind October's bonus pace (66.5% and 75.8% of target with 80.6% of the month gone).

Marketing

  • Website leads fell 16.4% WoW to 97 (64 calls, 14 texts, 6 directions); sessions and search impressions also down double digits.
  • 22 new Google reviews this week (Culpeper 8, Roanoke 8); AI visibility index still stuck at 71%, down from 87% three weeks ago (see Decisions).
  • eBay: 365 active listings / $62,924, fees 16.5% of revenue; Harrisonburg's "Below Standard" seller flag (set Sep 21) still has no posted re-evaluation result.

Real Estate

  • Bald Rock: Camille Hathaway checked out today after a 4-night stay ($3,524 payout); Patrick M. checks in Oct 28 for 2 nights ($1,611 payout) — contract sent but not yet signed.
