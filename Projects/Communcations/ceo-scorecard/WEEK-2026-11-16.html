Revenue & Sales

  • Net revenue $80,223 Bravo-basis, +104.5% week over week and +111.8% year over year - driven by gold and silver refinery settlements ($66,971 sold, up from $3,413 last week); nearly every store's single best day fell on Tue Nov 17.

  • November month-to-date $162,243, 62% of the $261,891 target with 73% of the month gone - Lexington is furthest behind at 52% of its $28,597 target.

Loans

  • Wrote $38,031 in new loans (217 loans), down 10.3% from last week but up 28.1% from a year ago; redeemed $22,694 (109 loans).

  • Loan book $750,763, down 0.4% on the week, up 22% YoY ($616,057); past-due % unreported since Oct 12 (dark 6 weeks).

  • Missed-call callbacks ran 3-16 a day, worst at Harrisonburg/Lexington; items-to-price backlog fell from 267 to 144 items this week.

People
