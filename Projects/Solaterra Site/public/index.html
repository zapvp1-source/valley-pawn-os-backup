Stump Grinding

Stumps ground flat, root flare included.

Old stumps take up space, wreck mower blades, and never fully rot away on their own. We grind them below grade so the ground is usable again — no digging, no dozer, no mess left behind.

Get a Free Stump Quote
The Process

What stump grinding actually involves

A mechanical grinder chews the stump down 4–8 inches below grade, root flare included — unlike full removal, grinding doesn't tear up the surrounding soil, sprinkler lines, or landscaping.

  1. Free estimate — from a photo or an on-site look, priced before anything starts.
  2. Site check — we flag anything nearby worth protecting: lines, hardscaping, irrigation.
  3. Grinding — the stump goes below grade, flush and walkable.
  4. Cleanup — grindings hauled off or left as mulch, your call.

FAQ

Stump grinding, answered

Do you remove the whole tree, or just the stump?
We specialize in the stump. If the tree's still standing, tell us and we'll help you sequence the work.
Will grinding damage my lawn or driveway?
We size the approach to work close to structures without unnecessary damage, and walk the site with you first.
Can I plant right on top afterward?
Usually, once the ground settles — ask about a deeper grind if you're planting immediately.
Do you handle rural, multi-acre properties?
Yes — from one backyard stump to dozens left over from a larger clearing job.

Forestry Mulching

Overgrown land, opened up in one pass.

Brush, saplings, and vines mulched right where they stand — no burn piles, no hauling, minimal ground disturbance. The fastest way to reclaim acreage that's grown up over the years.

Get a Free Mulching Quote
The Process

What forestry mulching actually involves

A track-mounted machine with a rotating mulching head grinds standing brush and small-to-mid trees into mulch in a single pass. It stays on-site, breaks down naturally, and helps control erosion.

  1. Walk the property — or review photos/coordinates — to scope what's being cleared.
  2. Free estimate — priced by acreage and density, given upfront.
  3. Mulching — brush and small trees cleared in place, pass by pass.
  4. Walkthrough — we review the finished area together.
Max growth sizeUnderbrush, vines, trees up to ~8–10" diameter
Debris handlingMulched in place — nothing hauled off

FAQ

Forestry mulching, answered

Will this work on steep or uneven ground?
Our equipment is track-mounted and built for rough East Tennessee terrain, including grades a wheeled machine can't handle safely.
Is mulching better than burning or bulldozing?
For brush and understory clearing, yes — faster, keeps topsoil intact, skips burn permits and hauling.
Will stumps be left behind?
Mulching handles brush and smaller trees at ground level; larger stumps can be handled separately with our stump grinding service.
How much land can you clear in a day?
Depends on density and terrain — we'll give a realistic timeline as part of your estimate.
