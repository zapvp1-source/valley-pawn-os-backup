## 5. Lease draft review — checklist (Claude runs this on the draft; flag only the real issues)

- Parties: tenant = Full Circle Finance Inc, a Virginia corporation **authorized to transact business in Florida** (qualification must be done before signing or lease should permit assignment to the qualified entity); DBA Valley Pawn.
- Premises SF measured how (BOMA?) — CAM pro-rata share stated as a fraction with denominator = total leasable SF of the center.
- Commencement vs rent commencement vs possession dates; free-rent language; delivery condition + punch list + landlord work letter.
- Permitted use (above); hours clause must allow 10–6 / Sat 10–5 / Sun closed (and Sunday if later chosen); no "continuous operation" requirement.
- CAM definition, caps, exclusions, audit rights, reconciliation timing; taxes and insurance pass-through; management fee cap.
- Repairs: landlord = roof/structure/exterior/parking; tenant = interior + HVAC maintenance only (replacement = landlord, or capped at $X/yr).
- Assignment/sublet to affiliate; change of control not a default.
- Default/cure: 10 days monetary, 30 days non-monetary with extension; no acceleration beyond mitigation.
- Security: right to install bars/grille/film/cameras/safes; vault floor-load.
- Signage exhibit; exclusive; relocation; co-tenancy; holdover 125% not 200%.
- Guaranty: cap, burn-off, no spouse signature (Hillary is not an officer).
- Governing law FL; venue St. Johns; radon disclosure (F.S. 404.056 — FL commercial leases must include it); brokerage disclosure.
- Insurance exhibit matches what our carriers will issue.
