St. Augustine store — name & logo concepts

Two finalists, three logo directions each, with a tagline per direction. Every mark is original vector art drawn for this review; the first concept in each set is the one the website mockups use.

Finalist 1Rocket Pawn & Jewelry

Distinctive, fast to say, nothing like it on the First Coast. Navy, rocket orange and a gold accent for the jewelry side of the business.

Launch Mark

“Fair deals, lightning fast.”

Diamond badge (jewelry) with the rocket on a 45° climb. Works as an app icon, a pylon panel and an embroidered polo. Primary recommendation.

Orbit Seal

“Where your money takes off.”

Round seal with an orbit ring and a gem at apogee. Strong on a window decal, a receipt header and social avatars.

Exhaust Wordmark

“St. Augustine's launchpad for loans, gold and great finds.”

Type-only with an exhaust underline. Reads at highway speed on a fascia sign and needs no icon to carry it.

Finalist 2Lighthouse Pawn & Jewelry

Borrows the county's most recognized landmark. Deep sea navy, beacon amber, coastal teal. Reads as steady and local before a customer ever walks in.

Beacon Tower

“Your guiding light on the First Coast.”

The spiral-striped tower is a direct nod to the St. Augustine Light, the most recognized landmark in the county. Primary recommendation.

Beam Seal

“Steady. Honest. Here when you need us.”

Round seal: the beam lights a diamond. Reads as a trust mark on receipts, decals and review-request cards.

Tower Wordmark

“A fair deal you can see from anywhere.”

The L is the tower. Type-only, one color if needed, built for the fascia sign and embroidery.
