Rocket Pawn & Jewelry — eight colorways

Same three logo concepts, eight palettes (E–G are the lime set; H is the Valley Pawn family palette for comparison). Each panel shows the primary mark, the seal, the wordmark, and the wordmark reversed on the brand's dark color (how it sits on a navy fascia sign or the website header). The website mockup has a switcher at the top so you can see each palette on the full page.
