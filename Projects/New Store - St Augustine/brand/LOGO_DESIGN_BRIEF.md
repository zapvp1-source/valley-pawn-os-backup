# Logo Design Brief — Rocket Pawn & Jewelry (St. Augustine, FL)

Prepared 2026-11-01 for a 99designs contest or a single designer engagement. Everything a designer needs is here; attach the four reference images in `brand/ai/` and the colorway/type boards.

## The business

Rocket Pawn & Jewelry is a new pawn shop and jewelry buyer opening spring 2027 at 2493–2495 US Highway 1 South, St. Augustine, Florida. It is the sixth store of a family-owned company that has run Valley Pawn (five stores in Virginia, 4.9-star average) since 2014. Services: pawn loans (no credit check), buying gold, silver, diamonds and coins, retail of new and pre-owned goods with a 30-day warranty, layaway, FFL firearms. Customers are working families, trades, service workers, retirees and military families. The brand promise is fair, fast, no-judgment, and we stand behind what we sell.

## What the logo must say

1. **Pawn shop and jewelry store first.** A stranger driving past at 45 mph should know what the store is. Jewelry/pawn cues welcome: faceted diamond, ring, the traditional three-sphere pawnbroker symbol, a tag/ticket. 
2. **"Rocket" is the name, not the subject.** A small nod to speed or lift is fine (a trail, a spark, an upward line). Do not make it a space company, a rocket illustration, a planet, or anything NASA-like.
3. **Trustworthy and modern, not flashy or "fast cash."** No dollar signs, no cartoon mascots, no distressed/grunge textures, no clip-art stars.
4. **Legible everywhere:** 20-ft fascia sign, pylon panel, window vinyl, 48px app icon, thermal receipt header, embroidered polo, Google Business Profile avatar. The mark must work as (a) full lockup with "& Jewelry", (b) stacked, (c) icon alone, (d) one color, (e) reversed on dark.

## Colors (pick one direction or propose both)

- **Black & Red:** black #111111, red #C8102E, white. Heavy, confident, highway-readable.
- **Teal & White:** teal #19B5A3, black #111111 for type, white. Modern, distinctive against every other pawn sign in the area.

No gradients in the primary mark. Gold (#D4A62A) may appear as a small accent for jewelry if it earns its place.

## Typography direction

Bold, condensed or wide uppercase sans. Reference faces we've looked at: Saira, Bebas Neue, Russo One, Chakra Petch. Avoid scripts, serifs, and anything that reads "luxury boutique"; this is a pawn shop that sells jewelry, not a jeweler.

## Must avoid (legal)

Rocket Mortgage / Rocket Companies own ROCKET marks in lending. The logo must not resemble their mark (the red "ROCKET" wordmark with a swoosh/arc, or any rocket-silhouette-with-ring in their style). The more the mark leans on jewelry/pawn symbols and the less on a rocket, the safer it is.

## Deliverables

Vector source (AI/EPS/SVG), PNG at 2000px with transparent background, versions: full-color, one-color black, one-color white (reversed), icon-only, horizontal and stacked lockups; favicon/app icon at 512px; a one-page usage sheet with clear-space and minimum size; full copyright assignment to Full Circle Finance Inc.
