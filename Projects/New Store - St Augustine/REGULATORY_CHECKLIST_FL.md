## 1. Florida Pawnbroking Act — FDACS Pawnbroker License (F.S. 539.001; Rule 5J-13 F.A.C.)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Requirement | Annual license from FDACS Division of Consumer Services; **a separate license for each pawnshop** (539.001(3)(a)). Operating without one is a 3rd-degree felony (539.001(17)(a)). Cannot open until issued. |
| Fee | **$300 per location** initial and annual, plus **$28.25 background-investigation fee per new individual** fingerprinted (FDACS-10111). Nonrefundable. |
| Security | Either **net worth ≥ $50,000** (FL CPA statement, notarized net-worth affidavit, or — for a corporation — most recent federal tax return) **or** a **$10,000 surety bond / irrevocable LOC / CD** from a Florida bank, per license (539.001(4)(a)2.). Disqualifiers: felony or theft/fraud-type conviction, plea, or incarceration within 10 years (539.001(4)(a)3.–4.). | Transaction form | "Pawnbroker transaction form" (FDACS-approved). Since **7/1/2025 (ch. 2025-33 / CS/CS/HB 139) may be digital or print**; digital must be ≥12-pt font. | Hold period | Pledged and purchased goods held **30 calendar days** unaltered; purchased goods may not be sold during the 30 days (539.001(9)(c)). Honor LE hold orders (539.001(15)). |
| Reporting to law enforcement | Deliver forms for the prior business day **by end of each business day** to the appropriate LE agency, **or transmit electronically** if LE supplies software/shop has capability (539.001(9)(a)–(b)). Watch 2026 bill CS/CS/SB 1582 (statewide pawn data-sharing, eff. 7/1/2027 if enacted). |
| Firearms on pawn forms | Electronic firearm pawn records held by LE are subject to F.S. 790.335 purge limits (AGO 2004-52). No extra shop duty beyond the form. |
| Record retention | Copies on premises ≥1 year (539.001(9)(a)); may not destroy forms sooner than **3 years** (539.001(12)(c)). |
| Minimum age | No pawns/purchases from persons under 18 (539.001(12)(d)). |

## 2. Secondhand Dealer (Ch. 538 Pt. I) and Secondary Metals Recycler (Ch. 538 Pt. II)

- **Pawnbrokers do NOT register as secondhand dealers.** FL DOR form DR-1S: "Businesses doing pawnbroker or title loan activity do not register as secondhand dealers. They register with the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services under Chapter 539, F.S." DOR GT-800036 (R. 07/23) says the same. Outright buys at a pawnshop are "purchases" under 539.001 (same form, 30-day hold, LE reporting). Ch. 538 has no express pawnbroker exemption in 538.03(2) — this is DOR's published, long-standing interpretation.
  - A non-pawn buy-only store would need DOR secondhand-dealer registration: **$6 per location**, annual renewal $6 due Oct 1, via DR-1S after sales-tax registration (538.09(1)); fingerprints for controlling owners/officers; **holds 15 days** general goods, **30 days** precious metals/jewelry/art (538.06(1)(a)); report to LE within 24 hours (538.04). Ch. 538 Pt. I allows *more* restrictive local ordinances (538.17), unlike ch. 539.
- **Secondary metals recycler (Pt. II) does not apply** to buying gold/silver/platinum jewelry: 538.18(6) excludes "precious metals subject to regulation under part I" from "nonferrous metals." Applies only to scrap copper/aluminum/catalytic converters — do not buy these.
- Sources: https://www.floridarevenue.com/Forms_library/current/dr1s.pdf ; https://www.floridarevenue.com/Forms_library/current/gt800036.pdf ; https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599%2F0538%2F0538.html ; https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/secondhand_dealers_recyclers.aspx

## 7. Federal financial-crime compliance (brief)

- **BSA/AML:** pawnbrokers are "financial institutions" (31 USC 5312(a)(2)(M)) but **31 CFR 1010.205(b)(1)(i) exempts pawnbrokers** from the AML-program requirement; SARs voluntary. **Dealers in Precious Metals, Stones & Jewels rule (31 CFR 1027)** requires an AML program if purchases AND sales of covered goods each exceed $50,000/yr — pawn loans excluded, outright jewelry buys and scrap-gold sales count → extend the existing written program to the new store. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-B/section-1010.205
- **Form 8300:** cash >$10,000 in one or related transactions (24-hour window; also a pawn renewed over 12 months and redeemed), file within 15 days; annual customer statement by Jan 31. Not an MSB.
- **OFAC** screening; **TILA/Reg Z** on every ticket (already required by 539.001(8)); **Military Lending Act** 36% MAPR + DMDC covered-borrower check (FL's 25%/30 days exceeds MAPR → must screen); **GLBA privacy notice + FTC Safeguards Rule**. NPA federal booklet: https://www.nationalpawnbrokers.org/assets/2024/01/federal-regulations-booklet-FINAL-4-6-23.pdf

## 11. Florida sales tax treatment of pawn activity

- **Pawn service charges / interest: not subject to sales tax** — a pawn is a loan secured by pledged goods (539.001(2)); ch. 212 taxes sales/rentals of TPP and enumerated services; separately stated finance charges excluded (Rule 12A-1.021). No pawn-specific DOR TAA found — have Silverline confirm in writing.
- **Sales of forfeited and purchased merchandise: taxable retail sales at 6.5%** (surtax capped at first $5,000/item). Buys/forfeitures are not taxable acquisitions (held for resale). Scrap gold to a refiner = sale for resale — obtain refiner's Annual Resale Certificate. Layaway: tax when sale completes. eBay sales to FL addresses taxed at destination rate.
- https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/discretionary.aspx ; GT-800036 above.

## Critical path / sequence (working back from a Q1–Q2 2027 open)

1. **Now (Nov 2026):** Confirm jurisdiction (county vs city) and zoning for the parcel — Confirmation Letter ($30) or City zoning email before lease execution. Confirm with SJSO the pawn-reporting system and contact for FDACS-10111.
2. **Now → +2 wks:** Order VA SCC Certificate of Good Standing → file FL foreign qualification ($70) with FL registered agent. ~1–3 weeks.
3. **+2 → +4 wks:** Publish "Valley Pawn" fictitious-name ad once in a St. Johns County newspaper → file $50 registration.
4. **+3 wks:** DOR DR-1 (sales & use tax, reemployment tax) → DR-11 certificate. Add FL to workers' comp; set up FL payroll in Gusto (new-hire reporting, E-Verify).
5. **Lease signed / premises secured (needed for both FDACS and ATF):**
   - **FDACS pawnbroker license:** Livescan all officers/directors/managers (ORI FL922060Z), net-worth proof (1120-S), FL transaction form from Bravo, SJSO contact → file FDACS-10111 + $300 + $28.25/person. **60–90 days.**
   - **ATF Form 7 (Type 02):** paper filing with fingerprints/photos, CLEO copy → FFLC background → IOI interview at premises (storage in place) → **~60 days** average, plan 90. Then **FDLE FPP enrollment** (days) and Bravo/4473 set-up.
   - These run in parallel and are the long poles: **file both ≥90–120 days before target open.**
6. **Build-out:** sealed TI plans → permit with fire plan review (2–4 weeks/cycle) → construction → fire final → **CO**. Sign permit in parallel (10–30 days).
7. **After zoning approval + FDACS license:** County BTR ($18–$30) (+ City BTR first if in city). Needs the state license copy, so it comes **last**.
8. **Opening week:** posters; FPP live; 8300/OFAC/MLA/privacy procedures; LE reporting feed tested with SJSO; 6.5% tax on first sales.

Compressed timeline from lease signing to legally open: **~4 months** if FDACS and ATF are filed within two weeks of signing and build-out is light. For a March 2027 open, FDACS and ATF filings must be in by **early-to-mid December 2026**.
