South St. Augustine along US-1 (ZIP **32086**), with reach into 32084 (city/west), 32080 (beach/Anastasia), 32092 (World Golf Village/209 corridor), 32095 (Palencia/north), 32033 (Elkton), 32145 (Hastings), 32033/Flagler Estates, and Palm Coast 32137/32164 (no Valley Pawn competitor between). Messaging: fair, no-judgment loans; honest gold buying; quality used goods with a warranty; a modern shop that texts you back.

Competitive set (verify at T-60 with a live GBP pull — do not publish comparisons): existing St. Augustine pawn shops on US-1 S and SR-16, gold buyers on King St, Palm Coast pawn shops. Review their star ratings/complaints; our differentiators are warranty, texting, app, and transparency.

## 5. Launch offers (load in Bravo as named discounts so attribution is automatic)

- **Gold Rush bonus:** +10% over our quote on gold/silver sales, first 30 days (cap per customer). Code GOLD10-STA.
- **First Loan Thank-You:** $5 service-charge credit on first pawn redemption (or a reduced first-period charge where Bravo supports it; stays within FL 25%/30-day cap). Code FIRSTLOAN-STA.
- **Grand Opening 15% off** storewide merchandise, weekend only (excludes firearms/ammo, precious metals — consistent with eBay/pricing rules). Code GO15-STA.
- **Mailer offer:** bring the postcard → $10 off a $50 purchase or the Gold Rush bonus. Code EDDM-STA.
- **Review thank-you:** not an incentive for reviews (Google policy) — thank-you only, no reward tied to posting.
- **Referral:** refer a friend who opens a loan/purchase → $10 store credit each. Code REFER-STA.
- **Veterans & first responders day:** monthly 10% off merchandise.
