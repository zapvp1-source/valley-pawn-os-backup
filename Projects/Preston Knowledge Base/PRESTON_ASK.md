# What We Actually Need From Preston (and the team)

**As of 2026-09-07, after harvesting 219 sourced entries from 2022–2026 Slack.**

The point of this file is to keep the ask **small and specific**. Preston should never be
handed a blank page and asked to "write down what you know." Everything below is either a
yes/no on something he already said, or a genuinely tacit thing the harvest could not
reach.

## A. The ongoing ask — ~10 minutes a week, forever

One Slack message a week in `#preston-claude` with **up to 10 entries**. Each is something
he already said, quoted back with the date. He replies with a number and one of:

- **`ok`** — still correct, publish it
- **`no`** — wrong or no longer true, kill it
- **`<the right answer>`** — a one-line correction

That's it. No forms, no meetings, no documents. His answers write straight into
`verified.json` and the corpus rebuilds.

**Why this is the whole ongoing cost:** everything else is harvested automatically from
what he already types in the normal course of his day.

## C. The one ask of the team — a single behavior rule

**Deal and valuation questions go in a channel. Never a phone call, never a DM to Preston.**

That is the only change anyone needs to make, and it is already ~90% true — `#deal-questions`
and the five `*-funds` channels exist and are used. But phone calls and DMs are
unrecoverable: knowledge that leaves through a phone call is gone, and it is the same
question asked again next month by someone else.

Two things make the rule stick without nagging:

1. **The agent answers first.** Ask in the channel and you often get an answer in seconds
   instead of waiting on Preston. That is a better experience, not a worse one — which is
   what makes people actually do it.
2. **Preston can enforce it in one line:** "put it in the channel and I'll answer there."

Nothing else changes for the stores. No new tool, no new form, no new process.
