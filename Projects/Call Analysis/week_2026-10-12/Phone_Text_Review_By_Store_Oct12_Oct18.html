WHAT CUTS ACROSS EVERY STORE

  • Past-due holds are still being given everywhere 77 calls this week had staff holding or extending loans already past due, though that stopped Oct 1. Roanoke has 33, Culpeper 25, Harrisonburg 8, Waynesboro 6, Lexington 5. Staff also invented four different rules: a five-day grace period, a 15-day one, a two-month pull rule and a 75-day rule. Customers are being told things that are not policy.
  • Account details given to callers nobody verified 46 calls gave out loan, item or due-date details with no name plus birth date or ID check: Culpeper 19, Roanoke 12, Waynesboro 7, Harrisonburg 4, Lexington 4. Firearm details were confirmed to unverified callers on 3 calls (Culpeper 2, Roanoke 1). Staff often looked accounts up by phone number alone.
  • Sellers are not being worked Of 96 sell calls, only 20 had proper qualifying questions and only 5 included a loan offer. Staff gave 26 price quotes by phone without seeing the item: Harrisonburg 8, Culpeper 7, Roanoke 7. Examples: Xbox Series S at about 125 and a Remington 31 at about 150. Customers are leaving without hearing the loan option.
  • 73 missed calls in open hours, and almost no callbacks 73 of 554 calls during open hours went unanswered (13%). Waynesboro, Lexington and Harrisonburg lost 17-21% each. Across all five stores we called back only 14 times. Harrisonburg's median callback was about 4 days. Waynesboro missed all 9 calls on Wed 10/14. Culpeper and Roanoke are the steadiest at 7% and 9% missed.
  • Preston line is mostly internal talk, with problems Of 38 recorded Preston calls only 2 were customers. There were 3 personal calls, gossip and coworker badmouthing, and 7 stolen-property or police matters. A police-department firearm buyer was approved but not charged because Preston did not know how to take payment and software support had been silent four days. Card readers fail about 1 in 5 transactions. One call could not be graded.
