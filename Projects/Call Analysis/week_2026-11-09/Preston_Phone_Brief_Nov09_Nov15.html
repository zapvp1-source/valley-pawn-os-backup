  • Bravo how-tos for loans and layaways Six loan or policy calls plus layaway and partial-redemption questions. Examples: grace-period pickups adding next month's interest and storage (adjust each down by about half), renewing a loan with a higher principal (Uriah Tiglao), applying layaway credits to pawns, and the buyout on a $250 firearm loan. Fix: short Ask Valley Pawn entries for each, plus a policy line that managers can do partial redemptions and sell-to-buy deals on their own.

Another store charged a credit card on a pawn redemption. A staffer also asked how to get a lunch reimbursed. Fix: knowledge-base entries on re-running a card after a void, the Fortis debit option, split tender (no credit card on pawn lines), and meal reimbursement by transfer tender. Also give Culpeper its own terminal manager override.
  • Firearm paperwork and FFL steps Three calls: how to enter a bare barrel and bolt that came in as a transfer, how to process a denied pickup while keeping the loan active, and how Culpeper could download the FFL from the website when the shared inbox wouldn't open. Fix: step-by-step knowledge-base entries for each, including how to open the shared FFL inbox.
  • Small approvals, call-outs and late arrivals Preston approved a $10 deal (Martin Dowden), a lunch break (Martin Dowden), an ER call-out, and a late arrival (Joseph Epperly's call). Fix: remind managers in writing that deals this size are within the limits raised 10/8, that managers approve their own staff's lunches, and that call-outs and late arrivals go to the store manager or lead first.
  • Culpeper policy gaps and IT setup Culpeper asked about a return after the 30-day window and a dispute over how a $60 payment was applied to expired-loan TVs. Burnett also got stuck enrolling computers in device management. Fix: a policy line on late returns and courtesy fixes like a replacement cord, a written process for expired-loan and layaway payment disputes, and a screenshot guide for the MDM enrollment steps.
