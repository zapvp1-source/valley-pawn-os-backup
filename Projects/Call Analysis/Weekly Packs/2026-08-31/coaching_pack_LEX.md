## Three things to do differently this week

1. **Never make the company the villain.** "My bosses are coming down on me," "this is from the
   owner and the GM," "I'm the reason your stuff hasn't been expired." It buys one call's worth of
   goodwill and costs the customer's belief that our terms mean anything. Say the terms as ours.
2. **Name plus one non-public identifier, before anything** — and nothing about a firearm on the
   phone, ever, verified or not.
3. **Write down what we didn't have.** Call 129 is the most expensive miss of the week: a caller
   asked for .350 Legend, .400 Legend, .450 Bushmaster *and* a rifled 12-gauge, said outright he
   intended to buy a deer rifle once, and was told *"just keep checking back with us
   periodically."* No name, no number. That is four straight-wall rifles' worth of deer-season
   demand we let walk.
