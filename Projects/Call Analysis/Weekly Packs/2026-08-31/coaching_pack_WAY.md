## Three things to do differently this week

1. **Name plus one non-public identifier, before anything.** Five WAY calls disclosed balances or
   payoffs on a first name or voice recognition, and four of the week's ten HIGH items are here.
2. **Never confirm a firearm is on the premises.** Three separate calls named a specific pledged
   gun. This is the single highest-risk habit in the store.
3. **Stop narrating the company to customers.** Who quit, who's on vacation, who's covering alone,
   what management is "cracking down on," what a colleague says about them. Every one of those
   lines this week cost us either trust or safety.
