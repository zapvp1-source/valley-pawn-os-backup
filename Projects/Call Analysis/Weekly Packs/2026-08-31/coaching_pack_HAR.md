## Three things to do differently this week

1. **Name plus one non-public identifier, before anything.** Not a phone number the caller
   supplies, not voice recognition, not a surname. Fifteen HAR calls this week failed this. If the
   caller can't produce the second identifier, the answer is "come in with your ID."
2. **Never say a firearm is here.** Not the make, not the model, not "I see it on my end," not on
   a voicemail. If the account is verified and the customer is the borrower, they can be told to
   come in — the item is not discussed on the phone.
3. **Take the name and number every time we can't fill it.** Zero captures out of every unfilled
   request this week. Porter Cable angle grinder (Call 108), Xbox Series X (Call 177), cheap
   peripherals (Call 227) — three customers who told us exactly what to buy and left no way to
   call them back.
