Source read: `Every Call Counts - backup-pre-slide12-20261024.pptx` (14 slides). The live deck is on the Desktop; apply these by hand (replace the text in the same text box; keep fonts and layout).

**The two decisions**
1. Loans: a loan is due on its due date. We never mention a grace period, to anyone. Late fee on the 6th day past due. Internal timeline (staff only, read from Bravo): held through 30 days past due, eligible to pull on day 31, pull done by day 61; a first-payment default is expired at once. Customer asks how long we'll hold it: "Your loan is due on its due date." Never promise a hold or a date not read from Bravo; never "two months", "60 days", "75 days".
2. Account details: only the verified account holder (full name + date of birth + phone number on file, all three) or a person with a signed power of attorney on file. Never a spouse, relative or friend otherwise. Never confirm a firearm by phone.

**Speaker notes, replace entirely with:**
"This timeline is for us, not for customers. A loan is due on its due date, and there is no grace period. Never tell a customer about one. On the 6th day past due the late fee applies; read the amount from Bravo. We hold the loan through 30 days past due. On day 31 it is eligible to pull, and by day 61 the pull is done. Never promise a hold, never say two months, 60 days or 75 days, and never give a date you didn't read off Bravo. We don't take partial payments: it's the extension or the payoff. And never blame anyone: it's 'our policy is,' not 'my boss says.'"
