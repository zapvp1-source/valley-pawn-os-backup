## Coverage and method
- For each store I loaded the Inbox **Closed** and **Open** lists until I was past conversations whose last activity was before 9/21. I opened every conversation with activity 9/21 or later and scrolled each thread back to before 9/21.
- The lists are sorted by last activity. Waynesboro, Harrisonburg and Lexington were loaded to 260 items or more, and Roanoke's closed list to 300 items. At every store the tail of the list was a single block of "Sep 17" campaign or scheduled-message conversations. Conversations whose last activity came before 9/21 but that had in-range messages would have shown a later date, so I believe none were missed.
- **Gaps:**
  1. **Lexington:** one closed conversation (last activity Sat 9/26, last message "Okay" from Uriah) froze the Chekkit web app every time it was opened, so it could not be read.
  2. **Lexington:** the Open tab (Analytics shows 1 open conversation) could not be reviewed. After that freeze, loading the Lexington inbox froze the app.
  3. **All stores:** photo/MMS messages appear only as "[no text/media]". I could not see the images.
- Conversations with only a missed-call auto-text and no customer reply are counted as conversations with 0 inbound messages.
- **Response time** is measured from the first customer message in the range that needed a reply to the first human reply. It is clock time and includes overnight and closed days. Spam is excluded.
- **Automated** messages are: the auto-reply ("We will text you as soon as we can…" or "we have received your message…"), the after-hours "we're currently closed" reply, missed-call texts and review requests.
- **Human** outbound messages include staff-sent past-due and collection notices. These are template texts sent by staff: Culpeper 8 (Sandi), Lexington 4 (Uriah).
