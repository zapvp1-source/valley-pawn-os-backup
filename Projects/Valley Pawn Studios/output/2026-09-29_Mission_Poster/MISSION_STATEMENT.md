# Valley Pawn — Mission & The Valley Pawn Code
Adopted 2026-09 · Owner: Joshua Davis · Source files: Projects/Valley Pawn Studios/output/2026-09-29_Mission_Poster/

## Our Mission
To be the most trusted name in the Valley — treating every customer with honesty, fairness and respect, and earning their business every single day.

## The Valley Pawn Code
I. We Tell the Truth. — No exaggerations. No half-truths. No fine print. We are honest with our customers, with each other, and with ourselves.
II. We Play It Straight. — Every appraisal fair. Every deal by the book. We never cut corners, because a win that isn't earned isn't a win.
III. We Protect What's Entrusted to Us. — Every item, every dollar and every minute on the clock belongs to someone. We never take what isn't ours.
IV. We Lead With Kindness. — Everyone who walks through our door is treated with dignity and respect — no judgment, no exceptions.
V. The Customer Comes First. — Every decision starts with one question: what is right for the customer?
VI. Good Enough Never Is. — Mediocrity has no place here. We take pride in our work, we do it right, and we get better every day.

What's Right Is Right.
