## Retail Sales Academy: required online sales training for new hires (Phase 1 within 7 days) (operational, announced 2026-10-01 by Joshua)
- Effective: 2026-10-01
- Legacy P&P section: Employee Administration
- Number hint: 01.12

Each new hire receives a login and completes Phase 1 within 7 days of receiving it. Training is done during the employee's scheduled shift, during slow time, and is paid time worked.
