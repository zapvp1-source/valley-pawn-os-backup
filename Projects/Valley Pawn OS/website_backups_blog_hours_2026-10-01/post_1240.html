How Pawnshops Decide What an Item Is Worth

Before we get to specific items, it helps to understand what a pawn loan really is. A pawn loan is simply a loan secured by something you own — no credit check, and no impact on your credit score. We lend based on an item’s resale value, its condition, and current demand. That means the more desirable and well-maintained something is, the more we can offer. A good tip: bring the accessories, cords, cases, chargers, and any original paperwork you still have. Those extras genuinely raise an item’s value and can mean a bigger loan for you.

Jewelry and Precious Metals

Gold, silver, and platinum jewelry are among the most reliable items you can bring in. Their worth is anchored to the precious metals market, so even a broken chain or a single mismatched earring still has value by weight. Diamonds and gemstones can add more on top of that. Class rings, bracelets, and watches from recognized brands tend to appraise especially well. If you have jewelry sitting unworn in a drawer, it may be worth more than you realize.

Tools and Equipment

Power tools hold their value remarkably well, especially trusted brands like DeWalt, Milwaukee, and Makita. As fall projects and seasonal work ramp up across the Valley, quality tools stay in steady demand — which is good news when you bring them to us. Cordless drills, circular saws, generators, and air compressors are all welcome. Just bring the batteries and chargers when you can, since a complete, ready-to-use tool is worth more than one missing pieces.

Electronics and Musical Instruments

Newer smartphones, tablets, laptops, and gaming consoles are quick to appraise and popular with buyers, so they make solid pawn items. Musical instruments are perennial favorites too — guitars, amplifiers, keyboards, and brass and woodwind instruments all have devoted buyers, and a well-maintained instrument can be worth a great deal more than its owner assumes. And if you ever choose to buy something from us instead of pawning, remember that everything we sell comes with a 30-day warranty, so you can shop with real peace of mind.

A Few Things to Keep in Mind

Not everything holds value the way people hope. Very old electronics, generic or off-brand goods, and heavily worn items are harder to lend on. But here is the best part: you never have to guess.
