Spring is one of the best times of year to get out and enjoy everything the Shenandoah Valley has to offer. From festivals and live music to outdoor adventures and local markets, there's something happening in just about every corner of the Valley over the next couple of months. Here's a roundup of events near our five Valley Pawn locations to help you plan your weekends.

Waynesboro

Mark your calendars for the Virginia Street Arts Festival on May 16–17. This popular downtown event brings artists from across the region to create brand-new murals right on the streets of Waynesboro. It's a great family-friendly outing with live music, food vendors, and the chance to watch art come to life in real time. Waynesboro is also a prime spot for hiking the Blue Ridge — the Appalachian Trail runs right through town, and spring is the perfect season to hit the trails before the summer heat sets in.

Roanoke

Roanoke comes alive in spring. The Strawberry Festival at Elmwood Park in early May is a local favorite, featuring homemade strawberry shortcake, artisan vendors, live music, and plenty of activities for kids. Downtown Roanoke's farmers market is also back in full swing with fresh produce, baked goods, and handmade crafts every Saturday. And just outside the city, the Vinton Dogwood Festival (April 24–25) brings craft vendors, a car show, live entertainment, a free kids zone, and the famous Dogwood Strolling Parade.

Harrisonburg

Harrisonburg's downtown scene is buzzing with spring energy. The weekly Harrisonburg Farmers Market returns with local farms, food trucks, and live music. JMU's campus area hosts a variety of community events through April and May. For nature lovers, Shenandoah National Park is just a short drive east, and spring wildflower season along Skyline Drive is hard to beat. Pack a picnic and make a day of it.

Lexington

Lexington's charming downtown is worth a visit any time of year, but spring is especially nice. Stroll the historic streets, browse local shops, and grab lunch at one of the many locally owned restaurants. Outdoor enthusiasts can kayak or tube the Maury River, and the Virginia Horse Center hosts equestrian events throughout the spring season. Keep an eye on the Lexington community calendar for gallery openings, live theater at the Lime Kiln, and seasonal markets.

Culpeper

Culpeper's historic downtown is a destination in itself, with independent shops, restaurants, and a growing arts scene. Spring weekends often feature community markets, live music on the downtown stage, and events at local wineries and breweries in the surrounding countryside. The area's rolling hills and farmland are gorgeous this time of year, making it a great region for scenic drives, farm visits, and wine tastings.
