One of the most common questions we hear at Valley Pawn is, "Do you take this?" The short answer: we accept a lot more than most people expect. If your item has value, there's a good chance we can work with it — whether you're looking for a pawn loan or want to sell it outright.

Jewelry and Precious Metals

This is our bread and butter. We accept gold jewelry of all karats (10K, 14K, 18K, 24K), sterling silver, platinum, diamonds, and gemstones. Broken chains, single earrings, old class rings, estate jewelry — it all has value. We also buy gold and silver coins, bullion bars, and collectible currency. Even if a piece is damaged, the metal content alone can be worth good money.

Electronics

Laptops, tablets, gaming consoles (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch), smartphones, smart TVs, headphones, and cameras are all popular items we see every day. We look at the brand, model, age, and condition to determine value. Pro tip: if you have the original box, charger, or accessories, bring them along — complete packages typically get higher offers.

Tools and Equipment

Power tools are some of the most popular pawn items in our stores. Brands like DeWalt, Milwaukee, Makita, and Snap-on hold their value well. We accept drills, saws, impact drivers, air compressors, tool sets, welders, and more. If it helps you get work done, it's probably something we'll take a look at.

Musical Instruments

Guitars (acoustic and electric), amplifiers, keyboards, drums, brass instruments, DJ equipment — we see it all. Brand name instruments from Fender, Gibson, Martin, Yamaha, and others tend to hold strong value. Even older or vintage instruments can be worth more than you'd expect.

Sporting Goods and Outdoor Gear

Fishing rods and reels, bows, golf clubs, bicycles, kayaks, and camping equipment are all items we regularly accept. If you've got quality outdoor gear collecting dust in the garage, it could be worth bringing in for a free appraisal.

Other Items We Accept

Beyond the big categories above, we also look at collectibles, antiques, designer handbags, watches (Rolex, Omega, Tag Heuer, etc.), coins, currency, and more. Every item is evaluated individually based on its condition, brand, demand, and current market value.
