How to Spot Quality Used Tech

Shopping smart for pre-owned electronics comes down to knowing what to check. Before you buy, run through this quick mental checklist:

  • Power it on. The single best test is simply turning the device on and using it for a few minutes. Watch how quickly it boots, open a few apps, and check that the screen has no dead pixels or cracks.
  • Check the ports and buttons. Plug in a charger, test the volume buttons, and make sure headphone jacks, USB ports, and charging connections all work.
  • Inspect the battery behavior. On laptops and phones, ask whether the device holds a charge. A quick look at the battery health settings can tell you a lot.
  • Look it over physically. Light cosmetic wear is normal and often means a lower price. Deep cracks, water-damage indicators, or a swollen battery are red flags.
  • Confirm it’s ready to use. Make sure the device has been reset and isn’t locked to someone else’s account.
