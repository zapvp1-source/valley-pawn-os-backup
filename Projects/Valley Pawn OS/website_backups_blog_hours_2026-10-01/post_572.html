How to Shop Smart for a Father’s Day Gift

A few simple habits will make sure the gift you bring home is one Dad will actually love.

Have a price range in mind, but stay open. Pawn shop inventory changes daily, and you may find something far better than what you originally imagined for the same budget. We see customers walk in looking for a $50 gift and leave with a $200 item they paid $80 for.

Ask questions. We know our inventory, and we’re happy to walk you through how something works, what it’s worth new, and why we priced it where we did. There’s nothing predatory about us — we’d rather you leave happy and come back than oversell you on something that isn’t right.

Bring Dad’s specs if you can. If he prefers a certain tool brand, watch size, or guitar style, let us know. We’ll point you toward what we have and even keep an eye out for what you’re looking for if it doesn’t come up that day.
