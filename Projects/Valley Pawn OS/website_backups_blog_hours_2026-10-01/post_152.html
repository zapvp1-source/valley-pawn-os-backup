What Is a Pawn Loan?

A pawn loan is a short-term, secured loan where you bring in an item of value — like jewelry, electronics, tools, or musical instruments — and receive cash based on what that item is worth. The pawn shop holds your item as collateral while the loan is active. Once you repay the loan plus any fees, you get your item back. It's that simple.

Why Choose a Pawn Loan Over Other Options?

Pawn loans offer something that most other short-term lending options can't: zero risk to your credit. If life happens and you can't repay the loan, the worst-case scenario is that you don't get your item back. There's no debt collection, no calls from creditors, and no negative mark on your credit report. For many people, that peace of mind is worth everything.
