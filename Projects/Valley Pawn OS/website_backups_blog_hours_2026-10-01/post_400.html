What to Look for When Buying Pre-Owned Tools

Shopping for used tools is easy when you know what to check. Here are a few things to keep in mind when you visit Valley Pawn this spring.

First, check the overall condition. Look for signs of heavy wear like cracked housings, frayed cords, or excessive rust. A little cosmetic wear is normal and doesn't affect performance, but structural damage is a red flag. Second, test it if you can. At Valley Pawn, we're happy to let you power up a tool before you buy. Plug it in, check that the motor runs smoothly, and make sure all the moving parts work as they should.

Third, check for completeness. Does the cordless drill come with a battery and charger? Does the set include all its pieces? Complete sets hold their value better and save you from buying accessories separately. And finally, don't be afraid to ask questions. Our team knows tools, and we can help you figure out whether a particular item is the right fit for your project.
