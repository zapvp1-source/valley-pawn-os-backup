Tips to Get the Most for Your Gold and Silver

Know what you have. Check for stamps or markings on jewelry — common ones include 10K, 14K, 18K, 925 (sterling silver), and .999 (fine silver). Knowing the karat or fineness helps you understand the baseline value before you walk in.

Don't clean or polish items. It might sound counterintuitive, but cleaning coins or antique silver can actually reduce their value. Bring items in their current condition and let our team assess them properly.

Separate your metals. If you have a mix of gold and silver, sorting them ahead of time can speed up the process. Keep gold jewelry separate from silver pieces and coins.
