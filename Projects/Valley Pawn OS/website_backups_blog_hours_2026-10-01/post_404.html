Tips for Buying a Pre-Owned Instrument

Shopping pre-owned is a little different from walking into a music store and picking something new off the wall, but it doesn’t have to feel intimidating. For guitars, check the frets — worn frets can affect playability — and make sure the tuning pegs turn smoothly. Inspect the neck for any warping or bowing. A few cosmetic scratches on the body are completely normal and don’t affect how the instrument plays or sounds. For brass and woodwind instruments, test the valves and keys to confirm they move freely without sticking.
