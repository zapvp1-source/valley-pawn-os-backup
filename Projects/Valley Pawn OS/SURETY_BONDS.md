**⚠️ Important distinction this file initially blurred:** the locality issues a **Dealer's
License/Permit** (signed by the chief of police), and a **surety bond** is one of the prerequisites
for it. A store can hold a license while its bond situation is separately unclear. Both need to be
tracked.

  Commissioner of the Revenue, City of Lexington) emailed that **"The Chief of Police has signed
  the Dealers License for Precious Metals and Jewels within the City of Lexington"**, followed by a
  renewal reminder on **2020-01-24** with a "Precious Metals Dealer Application.PDF" attached. So a
  City of Lexington license was issued. **What we do NOT have:** any copy of the issued license,
  any evidence of renewals after 2020, and any bond — on **2024-06-18** SuretyBonds.com told Joshua
  flatly **"I am not showing one for Lexington"** (the original 2015 bond was written for
  **Rockbridge County** through the prior broker JW Surety; Joshua asked in March 2019 to switch it
  to Lexington City and nothing on file shows that completing). **Additional risk:** the license is
  location-tied and the store **moved from 439 E Nelson St → 125 Walker Street**, so even a valid
  old license may not cover the current address.
- **ROANOKE — a bond IS required, and it LAPSED nearly 2 years ago.** Confirmed via SuretyBonds.com's
  own product page and VA Code Ch. 41: the City of Roanoke requires a **$10,000** precious metals

## How to extend this file

Any time a bond is renewed, cancelled, or a new store needs one, update the table above with the
new term dates and drop the current PDF into `Valley Pawn OS/Surety Bonds/`. If SuretyBonds.com
sends a portal link instead of an attachment, note that in this file rather than letting the
renewal go unfiled — the pattern above (Waynesboro, Augusta County) shows this happens often
enough to be routine, not exceptional.
