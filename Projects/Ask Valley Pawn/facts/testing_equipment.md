# Testing equipment in use — Layer 6 facts (Joshua, 2026-10-09)

## Gold and silver testers we use
Every store still uses the Kee gold tester. The testing order: magnet first on any precious metal; sort jewelry by its markings; confirm with the Kee and acid. Coins and bullion bars get magnet, weight and the Sigma — the Kee is not reliable on bars, which is why we have the Sigma. Diamond, gold and bullion testers are put away in their labeled spot when not in use, never left on the counter.
