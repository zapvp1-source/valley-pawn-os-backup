4. **How does layaway work?** (phone, 25×) — Layaway is free: no interest or holding fee. Jewelry is 10% down over up to 10 months. Other items under $500 are 20% down over up to 3 months; $500 and over is 10% down over up to 6 months. A payment is due monthly; 60 days with no payment expires it to store credit. — issue: A team member quoted 30 then 90 days on a $700 item.  _(evidence: C43, T41, T42)_
5. **Can someone else pick up my gun or get details on my account?** (phone, 8×) — Firearms go only to the owner with ID after the background check. We discuss an account only with the verified account holder, who can call us or come in. — issue: Some staff gave loan and firearm details without verification.  _(evidence: C16, C15, C78, C65, C72)_
6. **How do I get my pawned gun back?** (phone, 3×) — Redeeming a pawned firearm means the transfer paperwork and a State Police background check again, as if you bought it. Bring ID.  _(evidence: C18, K97)_
7. **Do you buy phones, laptops, TVs or other electronics?** (phone, 20×) — Send photos or bring it in; we decide after testing and checking market value. Don't promise a price by text. — issue: One store said 'we don't do phones at all' while another buys phones and laptops. Reasons for passing vary.  _(evidence: C97, C3, C81, C86, A10)_
8. **Can you tell me if my stolen item was pawned to you?** (phone, 6×) — We can't confirm or deny. Please file a police report. All buys and loans are reported to law enforcement daily as required, and police can look it up. — issue: One team member told a caller there was nothing on file for a named person.  _(evidence: C38, C39, C40, C42, C28)_
9. **My gun won't fire or I want to return it.** (phone, 4×) — _no good answer seen_ — issue: Answers conflicted (no returns, gunsmith look, oiling the chamber, trade). A firearms remedy rule is needed.  _(evidence: C32, C34, C93, C58)_
10. **What is your return and warranty policy?** (phone, 5×) — Used items carry a 30-day return/warranty from purchase; later problems fall outside it, and a disclosed defect tested at sale is final. — issue: Some callers escalated with no remedy offered.  _(evidence: K2, K6, K7, C33)_
11. **How much will you give me for this item by text?** (text, 15×) — We can't give an amount by text. Send clear photos, brand/model, condition and whether you want to sell or pawn. 12. **How does a pawn loan work and what will it cost me?** (phone, 10×) — You borrow against an item and redeem it for the loan plus the charges printed on your ticket. 13. **Can you receive a firearm transfer, such as a gun released by another police department?** (phone, 3×) — Yes, every Valley Pawn store is a licensed FFL and receives transfers; the background check is done at pickup.
