Check these at the counter:
1. Get the reference (between the lugs at 12) and serial (between the lugs at 6, or on the inner bezel ring on newer ones). An altered serial means we can't take it.

## 30. I have a pokeman card. How do i evaluate
expected: answer · got: **answer** · PASS · 4.4s · 829 chars
retrieved: ind:pawn_industry_baseline:6, ind:pawn_industry_baseline:1, ind:pawn_industry_baseline:3
checks: none

Here's how to check it:
1. Identify it: set, card number (bottom corner), name, language, and edition. A "1st Edition" stamp, holos, and special rarities carry the value. Most common cards are worth very little.
2. Check condition under good light: corners, edges, surface scratches, centering. Any crease or bend drops the value sharply.
3. If it's graded (PSA, BGS, CGC), look up the cert number on the grader's site and make sure the card and grade match. Check that the case isn't cracked or tampered with.
4. Comp it on eBay sold listings (exact card and grade), TCGplayer, or PriceCharting. Use sold prices, not asking prices.

Also watch for fakes: wrong font or colors, flimsy or glossy stock, a flat-looking holo, or a card that lets a lot of light through under a flashlight. Any offer still needs a manager's approval.
