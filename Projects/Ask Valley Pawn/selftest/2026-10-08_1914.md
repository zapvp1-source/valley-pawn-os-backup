Virginia's gate comes first. A non-dealer can't buy more than one handgun in 30 days, so before the second or third gun is sold you have to verify and record one of these exceptions, or the sale is refused:
- Valid Virginia concealed handgun permit. Record the permit number.
- Virginia State Police multiple-handgun purchase certificate. It's good for 7 days, must be surrendered to you before the sale, and is kept on file at least 2 years.
- A handgun trade-in as part of the same transaction (one such transaction per day).
- Replacing a handgun stolen or lost in the last 30 days, with the police report attached.
- A law-enforcement officer or agency.

No exception means no sale of the extra guns, however it's papered afterward.

Once the gate is cleared:
1. Notify your Store Manager right away.
2. Complete the 4473 the normal way. The customer fills out Section B in their own hand, with ID checked and age 21 or over.
3. The ATF multiple-sale report (Form 3310.4) is due by close of business the same day.
4. Have the customer sign the Multiple Handgun Sales Acknowledgement Form. If they refuse, note it and tell the Store Manager. It's kept 5 years and never replaces the Virginia check or the 3310.4.

Never split the guns up to dodge the reporting. That's a federal violation and a terminable offense. Also watch for straw-buyer signs, like one person picking the guns while the other does the paperwork, or "it's for my buddy". If you see that, stop and call Preston.
