## 30. I have a pokeman card. How do i evaluate
expected: answer · got: **answer** · PASS · 5.3s · 827 chars
retrieved: ind:pawn_industry_baseline:6, ind:pawn_industry_baseline:1, ind:pawn_industry_baseline:3
checks: none

Here's how to check it:
1. Identify it: set, card number (bottom corner), name, language, edition. A "1st Edition" stamp, holos and special rarities carry the value. Most common cards are worth very little.
2. Check condition under good light: corners, edges, scratches, centering. Any crease or bend drops the value sharply.
3. If it's graded (PSA, BGS, CGC), look up the cert number on the grader's site and make sure the card and grade match. Check that the case isn't cracked or tampered with.
4. Comp it with eBay sold listings for the exact card and grade, plus TCGplayer or PriceCharting. Use sold prices, not asking prices.

Watch for fakes: wrong font or colors, flimsy or glossy stock, flat holo, blurry print, or too much light coming through when you hold a flashlight behind it. A person has to approve any number.
