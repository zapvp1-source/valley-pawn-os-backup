## 30. I have a pokeman card. How do i evaluate
expected: answer · got: **answer** · PASS · 3.7s · 811 chars
retrieved: ind:pawn_industry_baseline:6, ind:pawn_industry_baseline:1, ind:pawn_industry_baseline:3
checks: none

Here's how to check it:
1. Identify it: set, card number (bottom corner), name, language, edition. "1st Edition" stamps and holo or special rarity cards carry the value; most commons are worth very little.
2. Check condition under good light: corners, edges, scratches, centering. Any crease or bend drops value sharply.
3. If it's graded (PSA, BGS, CGC), look up the cert number on the grader's website and make sure the card and grade match. Make sure the case isn't cracked or tampered with.
4. Comp it on eBay sold listings (exact card and grade), TCGplayer or PriceCharting. Use sold prices, not asking prices.

Watch for fakes: wrong font or colors, flimsy or glossy stock, flat holo, blurry print, or a card that lets a lot of light through under a flashlight. The offer still needs a manager's approval.
