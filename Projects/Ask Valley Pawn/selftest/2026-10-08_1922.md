Good one to ask first. Virginia's gate comes before anything else. A customer can't buy more than one handgun in 30 days unless you verify and record an exception, or you refuse the sale. The exceptions you'll actually see:
1. A valid Virginia concealed handgun permit. Record the permit number.
2. A Virginia State Police multiple-handgun purchase certificate. It's good for 7 days, must be surrendered to us before the sale, and we keep it on file at least 2 years.
3. A trade-in of a handgun in the same transaction, limited to one such transaction per day.
4. Replacement of a handgun stolen or lost in the last 30 days, with a copy of the police report attached.
5. A law-enforcement officer or agency.

If none of those applies, don't sell more than the one. A sale that fails the gate isn't made, however it's papered afterward.

Once the gate is cleared:
1. Notify your Store Manager right away.
2. Complete the 4473 as usual. The customer fills Section B in their own hand, and you do A, C and E. They must be 21 or older with an unexpired government photo ID.
3. File ATF Form 3310.4 by close of business the same day. It's required for two or more pistols or revolvers to the same customer within five consecutive business days.
4. Have the customer sign the MHG Acknowledgement Form. If they refuse, note it and tell the Store Manager. It doesn't replace the Virginia gate or the 3310.4.
5. Keep the documentation in the store compliance file for at least 5 years.

Never split the sale up to dodge the reporting. That's a federal violation and a terminable offense. If it looks like they're buying for someone else (one person picks, another does the paperwork, "it's for my buddy"), stop and call Preston.
