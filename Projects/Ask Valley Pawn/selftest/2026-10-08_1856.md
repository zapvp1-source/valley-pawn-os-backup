Good question. Virginia's gate comes first, then the federal paperwork.

1. Virginia one-handgun-per-30-days: before the second handgun is sold to the same customer within 30 days, verify and record an exception or don't make the sale. For three at once, that means the customer needs one of these:
   - a valid Virginia concealed handgun permit (record the permit number)
   - a Virginia State Police multiple-handgun purchase certificate (good for 7 days, surrendered to us before the sale, kept on file at least 2 years)
   - a handgun trade-in as part of the same transaction (one such transaction per day)
   - replacement of a handgun stolen or lost in the last 30 days (attach the police report)
   - a law-enforcement officer or agency
   No exception, no sale. It can't be papered after the fact.
2. Once that's cleared, a 4473 for the sale, with the usual ID and age checks (21 for handguns). Customer fills Section B themselves.
3. ATF Form 3310.4 for two or more pistols or revolvers to the same customer within five consecutive business days. It's due by close of business that day.
4. Tell your store manager right away.
5. Have the customer sign the MHG Acknowledgement Form. If they refuse, note it and tell the store manager.
6. Keep the paperwork in the compliance file for at least five years.

Don't split the sales to dodge the reporting. That's a federal violation and a terminable offense. And if it looks like he's buying for someone else, stop and call Preston.
