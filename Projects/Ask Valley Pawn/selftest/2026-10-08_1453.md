In Virginia the State Police run the check (VCheck), not ATF or NICS directly. If it comes back delayed:
1. Delayed is not denied. The State Police just need more time.
2. The firearm stays in the store. It does not go out the door on a delay.
3. Virginia law gives the State Police until the end of the store's fifth business day, counted from when you submitted. Holidays like December 25 don't count.
4. What happens after that fifth business day is Preston's call. Don't make up a rule.
5. If the customer asks, say: "Let me get you the right answer from my manager." Then get it from Preston.
You can discuss check status by phone only with the buyer, after you've verified who they are. Never mention a gun, an approval or an account on a voicemail.
