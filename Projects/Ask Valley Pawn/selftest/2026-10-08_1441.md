In Virginia the State Police run the check, not ATF or NICS directly, but a delay works the same way:
1. Delayed is not denied. The State Police just need more time.
2. The firearm stays in the store. It does not go out the door on a delay.

4. What happens after that fifth day is Preston's call. Don't make up a rule or promise the customer a timeline like "come back tomorrow."
